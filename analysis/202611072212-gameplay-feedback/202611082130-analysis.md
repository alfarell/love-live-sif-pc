# G6: the finale section and its score

Date: 2026-11-08 21:30 (Asia/Jakarta). Step G6 of `plan/202611072212-gameplay-corrections/202611072212-plan.md`.

## Rules (`src/game/live/judge.{h,cpp}`)

- **`Judge::icons`:** counts icon notes pressed better than Miss. It replaces `CollectedIcons` from G5 (`finale.h` is removed), so the judge alone decides the finale.
- **`Judge::finale`:** decided once, when the clock (a press, a release or `Update`) reaches `Chart::finale_ms`. It is true if all nine icons are in by then.
- **Score:** `AddPoints` takes the note index. Notes at or after `finale_ms` score × `ScoreRules::finale_multiplier` while the finale runs. This covers their judgements and hold ticks.
  - `finale_multiplier` is read from `s_CommonParam` +0x120 (3.0) in `LoadScoreRules`.
  - It multiplies on top of the difficulty multiplier. G0 measured about ×3 of the plain points.
- **`MaxScore()`:** stays without the finale (*Hypothesis*, as planned). A finale play can pass 1.0.
- **`ClearRate()`:** capped at the top rank threshold (1.2). The game shows 120.0% for a score 1.48 × the maximum.

## Screen (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`)

- **Finale notes:** a note at or after `finale_ms`, while `judge.finale` is set, draws the group's yellow disc (`ui_ingame_notes` (0, 1) star, (1, 1) anchor, (2, 1) snowman) in place of the pink fill, under the ring and icons. Icon marks and brackets still draw on top.
- **Finale holds:** they use `connect_note_finale` for their band.
