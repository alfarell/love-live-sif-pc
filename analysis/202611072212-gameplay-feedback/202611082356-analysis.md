# H3 follow-up: bracket size from the user's PS4 still

Date: 2026-11-08 23:56 (Asia/Jakarta). The user confirmed that the note size is right and asked for the simultaneous-note brackets to be slightly smaller and closer to the ring, with two references: image 1 from the PS4 and image 2 from the port.

## Measured

The images were measured on 2× and 3× zoomed grids. The brackets hide the ring's sides, so the ring is measured vertically.

| | Bracket span (outer edges) | Ring, vertical outer diameter | Span ÷ ring |
|---|---|---|---|
| PS4 (image 1, 443 × 356) | 366 px | 290 px | **1.26** |
| Port (image 2, 283 × 211) | 246 px | 188 px | **1.31** |

The port's value agrees with its constants: the bracket cell's span (202 px) ÷ the note ring (163 px) = 1.24, times `kBracketScale` 1.05, gives 1.30.

**Target:** 1.26 / 1.24 → **`kBracketScale` = 1.02**.

H3 measured 1.27-1.33 in the shadPS4 frames. Those are small, blurred notes, and their outlines widen the span by a pixel or two. The user's still is larger and sharper, so it is the better reference.

## Change

`Playfield::kBracketScale` 1.05 → 1.02 in `src/app/live_screen.cpp`.

- **Applies to:** every simultaneous note, tapped or hold, in flight or held, because there is one bracket draw site. H4's tail brackets will use the same constant.
- **Unchanged:**
  - the brackets are still centred on the note;
  - their colours: `group % 2`, orange or blue;
  - the note size, the note assets, positions, timing and input.
