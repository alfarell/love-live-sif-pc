# H3 follow-up: note ring matched to the indicator's body

Date: 2026-11-08 23:48 (Asia/Jakarta). The user's follow-up to H3: the moving note's circular body must equal the circular element of "mark row 1 col 1". That is cell (0, 0) of `ui/ui_ingame.pac/texture.pac/ui_ingame_judgering_all_mark`, the indicator, counted from 1. The orange outline and the stars are excluded.

## Measured in the textures

Each texture was profiled down its vertical centre line. The centre row crosses the side stars, so it could not be used.

| Cell | Parts along the vertical centre line (rows of the 256 px cell) | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Mark (0, 0), the indicator | Orange outline 39-46; grey and silver body **47-209**; orange outline 210-216 | Body **163 px**; with the outline 177 px |
| Notes (1, 0), the note ring | Alpha above 128 over **47-209** | **163 px** |

The note ring and the indicator body have the same size and position in their cells. So they match exactly when both cells are drawn at the same scale, and the indicator is drawn 1:1 (a 256 px quad).

The port drew notes at `kNoteSize` = 0.95 × 256, so at arrival the ring was 155 px, 8 px smaller than the body. H3's game-frame measurement (101 vs 103 px at 1280) could not resolve this: it is under 3 px at 1280 and within JPEG blur.

## Change

`Playfield::kNoteSize` = `kCell` (256): at the ring a note is drawn 1:1, like the indicator.

**Unchanged:**
- H3's growth curve: 0.8 at the mic to 1.0 at the ring;
- `kBracketScale`;
- positions, approach time, judging.

Everything drawn on a note uses the same quad, so all note types scale together:
- the fill;
- the ring;
- the icons;
- the finale disc (cells (0-2, 1));
- the icon mark;
- the brackets.

Hold bands (a fraction of the quad) and their caps grow by the same 5%.
