# H4: brackets at simultaneous hold tails

Date: 2026-11-09 00:03 (Asia/Jakarta). Step H4 of `plan/202611072212-gameplay-corrections/202611082306-plan.md`.

## Rule (rule A of `202611082306-analysis.md`)

`GroupNotes` in `src/game/live/chart.{h,cpp}` now groups two kinds of event:
- **heads:** presses within 1 ms of the previous head;
- **hold tails:** releases (`end_ms`) within 1 ms of the previous tail.

A group needs two or more members, and the two kinds never mix.

All groups are then numbered 0, 1, 2… in time order; at equal times a head group comes before a tail group. A note's head group is `Note::group`; its tail group is `Note::end_group`, which is new. The bracket colour is the number % 2: even is orange, odd is blue.

What this gives:
- **Tap pairs:** numbered as before, so their orange/blue alternation is unchanged when no hold tail falls between them. The old unit case is unchanged.
- **One hold pair:** its heads and tails always get opposite colours.
- **Consecutive hold pairs:** each goes head orange → tail blue, the user's table.
- **A group between a hold pair's heads and tails** takes the next number, so the colours keep alternating in time.

**Edge cases chosen** (*Hypothesis*: no game frame shows them):
- two holds that start together but end apart get no tail brackets;
- a tail and another note's head at the same time are not one group.

## Drawing (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`)

- `Playfield::Tail` carries the tail's group.
- `DrawBands` draws `bracket[group % 2]` on the end cap, after the cap.
- **Size:** the cap is treated as a note ring. The bracket quad is `cap diameter / (163/256) × kBracketScale`, so the brackets sit on the cap the way they sit on a note.
- The brackets stay while the hold is held, together with the band, as in image 4.
