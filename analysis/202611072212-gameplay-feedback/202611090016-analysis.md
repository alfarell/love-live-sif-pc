That is five sounds per section, one per grade. None is shared between grades.

The finale set is used for notes at or after `finale_ms`, once the finale is on (`Judge::finale`). *Hypothesis:* this is the eboot's column switch (`[ctx+0x60]+8 == 4`).

## Track commands, read for these cues

Command lists are `u16` opcode, `u8` size, data.

| Opcode | Where | Values | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| `0x006F` | Every list | Bus index 0-7, level 10000 | Bus sends at 100% |
| **`0x0057`** | Tracks | 41 (Perfect), 27 (Great), 52 and 30 (Good), 34 and 31 (Bad), only on the extra layers | **Read as the track's volume in percent** (*Hypothesis*) |
| `0x0005`, `0x0079` | Sequences and synths | 165 / 120 / 60 (Perfect / Great / Good), 300 (finale) | Not used. *Hypothesis:* a priority |
| `0x0022` | Synths | 100 | Not used |

Resulting layers, as wave (MemoryAwbId) and gain:

| Cue | Layers |
|---|---|
| Perfect | 184 at 100%, 249 at 100%, 46 at 41% |
| `miss` | 204 alone |
| `_fl` cues | Add the 4-second finale wave 195 |

The source waves are a mix of 48 kHz and 44.1 kHz, mono and stereo, all ADX.

## Code

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `engine/audio/cue_sheet.{h,cpp}` | `Layer`. `FindLayersById(cue_id)` collects every wave a cue plays together: all tracks of a sequence, and all items of a polyphonic synth (type 0; other synth types give their first item). The gain comes from `TrackGain` (opcode `0x0057`); the `TrackCommandTable` is now parsed. `FindWave` and `FindWaveById` are unchanged. |
| `engine/audio/sound_bank.{h,cpp}` | `LoadCueMix(cue_id, rate, channels)` decodes the layers and converts each to the target format: linear resampling, mono copied to every channel, extra channels averaged. It sums them with their gains and clamps to 16 bits. |
| `engine/audio/audio_output.{h,cpp}` | `PlayEffect(const Pcm*)` and `StopEffects()`: 8 effect slots under the stream lock, as the voice already uses; a free slot is taken first, else the oldest. The callback adds active effects onto the music chunk it is about to feed, clamped. **`fed_frames_` and the clock still count music frames only**, so the master clock and judging timing are unchanged. `Play()` clears the effects. |
| `app/live_screen.cpp` | Before the music starts, the ten cues are mixed into the music's format. The judgement log loop plays the cue of each new judgement, at most once per cue per frame. Releases and Misses play too. `StopEffects()` runs before the sounds are freed. |

**Shortcuts:**
- **Duplicates:** two notes judged the same in one frame give one sound.
- **Hold releases:** a release plays its judgement sound like a press.
- **Latency:** sounds start on the next frame after the judgement, plus the device buffer (10 ms here).
- **Volume:** the master volume only; there is no sound-effect volume option.

The game's rules for duplicates and releases are not traced. The caller of `0xB06C0` is a function pointer.
