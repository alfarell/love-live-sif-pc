## 1. Channels 2-4 are extra notes; one channel per phase carries the icons

**Eboot.**
- The live loader at `0x8F670` opens `_ch1`, then the channels in the table at `0x5E7830`: **2, 3, 4, 6, 7**.
- It hands every one of them to the same note builder (`0xA08B0`, then `0xA0D80`).
- `_ch5` is opened separately (`0x98A50`, channel argument 5) and goes to another object (`0xABAC0`). So channel 5 is a timeline, not notes.
- The per-channel file name comes from `0xA8760`, using `score_%04d_%02d` and `_ch%d`.

**Note counts** (run 0, no input):
- The game's result shows **Miss 194**.
- The chart has 167 `ch1` notes (39 of them holds) plus 3 × 9 notes in `ch2`-`ch4`: 167 + 27 = **194**.
- So all 27 channel 2-4 notes are played as ordinary extra notes, and an unpressed hold is one Miss (as in the port).

In run 1 the result is **Perfect 233**: 167 + 27 presses plus 39 hold releases.

This also explains the open M4 question: "song 0340 Easy has 101 notes and 20 holds (121), yet the result screen totals 128 (no input)". 101 + 27 = 128.

**Which notes carry the icon** (the group's two-symbol mark):

| Phase (`ch5` times) | Run 0 | Run 1 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (notes 60/63/66) | `ch2` (← at 72.8 s, × at 78.2 s) | `ch2` (←) |
| 2 (61/64/67) | `ch4` (→□ at 97.4 s) | `ch4` (→□) |
| 3 (62/65/68) | `ch3` (↓ at 114.2 s) | `ch3` or `ch4` (a ← note; the timing does not separate them) |

The notes of the other two channels in a phase are plain extra notes.

*Hypothesis:* the set per phase is fixed (ch2, ch4, ch3) or chosen per phase by a rule not yet found. Runs 0 and 1 agree for phases 1 and 2. The port can use ch2/ch4/ch3 as a marked shortcut until a trace of `0xABAC0` shows the rule.

## 2. The LOVELIVE! letters and the finale

- **Phase start.** At each phase start (the `ch5` time), the nine HUD marks flash yellow. In run 0 this was at burst 70.3 s, which was predicted from the chart.
- **Collecting.** Each icon hit lights the next mark with its letter (L, O, V, E, L, I, V, E, !), on the group symbol (`ingame_ffmark_ms`). See `work/gameplay-fix/r1_score.png`.
- **Missing.** In run 0 (all missed) the marks stayed grey all song and **no finale happened**: the last section looked normal. Whether one missed icon is enough to lose the finale was not tested (Q5 partly open).
- **Finale.** In run 1, with all nine letters lit, everything changed from the `ch5` note-69 time (audio 108.7 s):
  - **notes:** the yellow group disc (`ui_ingame_notes` row 1), with the pink ring, the button icon and small stars;
  - **indicators:** a gold glow;
  - **particles:** rainbow stars (`ui_particle_finale_symbols`) at the edges.

  See `work/gameplay-fix/r1_fin.png`.

## 3. Finale score

- **Plain all-Perfect score.** Port formula (multiplier 1.5 for Normal) over all 194 notes and holds: **39,975**.
- **Game, run 1:**
  - HUD score at the end of the song: **49,865**;
  - result screen: **59,215**, clear rate **120.0%**, rank SSS.
- **Fit of the HUD over time** (`work/gameplay-fix/fit.py`, best audio offset 3.88 s; reading error about ±500):
  - **Before phase 1:** the HUD matches the formula.
  - **Phases 1-3:** about +2,000 builds up. *Hypothesis:* icon notes or letters give a small bonus.
  - **Finale:** from 108.7 s the HUD gains about 11,400 against 3,480 plain points: about **×3**. This fits `s_CommonParam` +0x120 = 3.0, the multiplier used when check `0x93D00` is true (U5 notes). *Hypothesis:* `0x93D00` is "finale active".
  - **After the song:** +9,350 more is added between the HUD and the result screen. Source unknown. Candidates are the bonus counters `s_CommonParam` +0x1B4-0x1BC (2000 / 6000 / 30000) or an ALL PERFECT bonus.
- **Clear rate** shows 120.0% although the score is 1.48 × the plain maximum. So the clear rate is **capped at 120%**, the top rank threshold.

Q2 is answered by measurement (×3), not by a trace.

## 4. Holds (Q4)

Frames: `r1_hold_zoom.png`, `r1_double.png`.
- **Before the press:** a translucent **white tapered band** runs from the note head back to a **white end disc**. The disc is smaller because it is further along the path.
- **While held:**
  - the head stays on the ring;
  - the band and the end disc turn **translucent pink**;
  - the end disc travels down the path to the ring.
- **At release:** the judgement text appears and the band is gone.
- The tail end is a disc, so the port's end disc stays; it gets the white/pink colour.

## 5. Simultaneous notes (Q3)

- **Brackets.** Each note of a group has the bracket pair and short side bars. Seen colours in order:
  - the first L1/R1 pair: **orange**;
  - the next pair: **blue**;
  - later pairs alternate.

  This confirms the alternation.
- **Pressed or held together.** Both notes sit on their rings, keep their brackets, and show yellow sparkles. **No separate vertical line appears** in any frame of run 1 (the 7.2 s double hold at 22.5 s is the clearest case). The vertical parts of the brackets are the only vertical lines. Please confirm whether those are the "vertical line" you meant.

## 6. Judgement text and combo (Q6)

- **Judgement text:** each judgement plays at **its own ring**. The text arches over the top of the ring with a ring burst. Two lanes judged at once show two texts.
- **Combo:**
  - translucent (about half opacity) orange digits with "COMBO";
  - centred at about **(960, 720)** on the 1920 × 1080 canvas, which is lower than the port's 470;
  - from the first note ("1 COMBO").

## 7. New finding: the game sometimes plays a chart mirrored

- **Today:** all three runs (Normal twice, Easy once) were mirrored: note n went to lane 118 − n. For example, Easy's first notes 57, 61, 58 landed on ○, ←, ×.
- **31 Oct (M4):** the same song on Easy was not mirrored. Its first note 57 landed on ← (`work/shadps4-run/live-23.png`).
- **Records:** `s_live_datas` has no flag that differs.

*Hypothesis:* a random or rule-based mirror per play or session (arcade-style "mirror" variation), or a skill or option. The port keeps the chart's own order. Mirroring is noted for later.
