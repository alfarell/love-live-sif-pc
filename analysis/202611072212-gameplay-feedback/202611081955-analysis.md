## Code (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`, `Playfield`)

- **`Path()`:** the note path, shared by notes and bands.
- **`LoadBands`:** loads `common/common.pac/common_tex.pac/connect_note` and `ui_ingame_common_circle_05`, and creates 32 dynamic quads.
- **`SetBands`** (each frame, before it starts) builds one tapered quad per hold on screen:
  - from the head (pinned on the ring while held) to the tail;
  - width = 0.8 × the note ring's pink diameter at each end.
- **`DrawBands`:** draws the bands at 62% and the end discs (the solid core of `circle_05`, as wide as the band), white, or pink while `Judge::Held`. They are drawn before the notes, so they sit under them.
- **Removed:** the disc trail and the old end fill.
- **Kept:** the pulsing `ui_ingame_longnote_circle_01` glow on the ring while held.
