# A hold ending on another note's press: brackets on both

Date: 2026-11-15 14:57 (Asia/Jakarta). User feedback "Simultaneous Hold Note Release Indicators", with a PS4 still of 0010 HYPER: a hold's end cap and a note to be pressed at the same moment both wear brackets.

## What the port did

- **Grouping** (`GroupNotes`, `src/game/live/chart.cpp`): presses within 1 ms formed a group, and so did hold ends (tails). Presses and tails were never put in one group. The open question in `202611082306-analysis.md` ("whether a hold tail at the same time as another note's head counts as one event") had been left that way.
- **Result:** a tail that ends alone with one press got no group on either side, so neither the end cap nor the press had brackets.
- **Drawing** was already right: a press with `Note::group >= 0` gets the brackets on the note (`RunLive`), and a tail with `Note::end_group >= 0` gets them on its end cap (`Playfield::DrawBands`). Only the grouping was missing.
- **Judging** never reads the groups. The press and the release are judged on their own windows (`s_EvaluateRangeDatas` group 0 for presses, group 1 for releases), and each counts once. That is unchanged.

## How often it happens (channel 1 of every chart in the dump, `work/m11/tail_survey.py`)

- **Total:** 26,018 moments where a hold ends on the tick of another note's press, in every difficulty (EASY 42 … CHALLENGE 7,361; home charts too).
- **Mix of tails and presses** at those moments (tails, presses): (1, 1) 15,498; (2, 2) 4,966; (2, 1) 2,441; (1, 2) 2,433; (1, 3) 466; (3, 1) 214.
- **Lanes:** none has a press on the hold's own lane (`work/m11/same_lane.py`), so there is no chained same-lane case to treat apart.

## Change

**`GroupNotes`:** one time-ordered list of presses and tails. Every chain of 2+ events, each within 1 ms of the previous, is one group, numbered 0, 1, 2 … in time order.
- **Kept:** pure press groups and pure tail groups get the same numbers as before relative to each other. A hold pair still has orange heads and blue tails (rule A), and a tap pair between a hold pair's heads and tails still takes the next number.
- **New:** a tail and the presses at its moment share one number, so the end cap and the notes wear the **same** colour. This matches the PS4 still, where both are blue. The colour alternation over groups is unchanged.
- **Hypothesis:** the game uses the same 1 ms window for mixed groups. All 26,018 cases are exact tick matches, so the window does not matter for these charts.

**Tests** (`tests/unit/live_test.cpp`): a tail plus one tap; a tail plus two taps; a hold pair's tails plus a tap, after the heads' group. The old check "a tail and a head at the same time do not form a group" is removed, since it encoded the old rule.

## Checks

- **Capture:** 0010 HYPER, autoplay, frames every 150 ms around 13.3 s (`work/m11/shots`, `work/m11/t04_crop.png`). The ↓ hold's end cap and the R1 press both show orange brackets; the next group is blue. Bracket size and placement are the existing ones.
- **Scenarios:**
  - a plain hold has no brackets (group -1);
  - a tail plus one press: one group;
  - a tail plus several presses: one group (unit test; 466 such moments in the charts);
  - two tails plus presses: one group;
  - the colour alternates over groups.
- **Judging:** the autoplay run ends Perfect 37 of 37, Miss 0 (presses and releases each judged once).
- **Unit tests and routes:** 16 of 16 unit tests and 25 of 25 routes pass (Debug). The Release exe was open (the user's session), so it was not rebuilt.
- **User check:** the user tested the Release build and confirmed it looks good; the checklist item is ticked.
