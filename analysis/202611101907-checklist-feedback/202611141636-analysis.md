Date: 2026-11-14 16:36 (Asia/Jakarta). Step F2 of `plan/202611101907-checklist-fixes/202611101907-plan.md`. The game's timeline is in `202611101907-analysis.md` §2.

## Change (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`)

**The result text.** At the last judgement the port picks:

| Text | When |
|---|---|
| **ALL PERFECT** | the play is a full combo and has no Great |
| **FULL COMBO** | no Good, Bad or Miss (the same rule `main.cpp` uses for the records) |
| none | otherwise |

The log line `live end: <text>, LIVE CLEAR in N s` records the choice.

**The texture.** `ui_ingame_end_completion` is loaded with two quads, one over each half: FULL COMBO on top, ALL PERFECT below. `DrawFad(..., text_half)` plays the LIVE CLEAR animation with its text sprite (texture `ui_ingame_end_text_liveclear`) drawn as that half instead. The half keeps its own aspect at the sprite's width, centred on the sprite. The rings, notes and shine stay as they are. *Hypothesis:* the game swaps the texture the same way; its ALL PERFECT frames move like LIVE CLEAR.

**Timing**, counted from the last judgement on the wall clock:

| Step | Port | Game (frames, run 1) |
|---|---|---|
| ALL PERFECT / FULL COMBO | 0 s (its 200 frames) | 0-2.6 s |
| LIVE CLEAR | 3.9 s, or when the music is audibly over if that is later | 3.9-6.3 s |
| The dance goes on | until 8.4 s | until 8.2 s |
| Fade to white | 8.4-9.4 s | 8.4-9.7 s (white, then black) |
| Fade to black (the screen fade), result | 9.4-9.7 s | about 10 s |

**The audible end** is the last music sample above 328, about -40 dBFS (decibels below full scale: a level near silence). It is found once, before playback. Without a full combo, LIVE CLEAR starts at that point (or at once, if the music is already over).
