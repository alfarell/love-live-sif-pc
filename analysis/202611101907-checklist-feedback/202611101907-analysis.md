## 3. Note speed

**Question:** is the port's "Note speed" setting the PS4's?

**No.** The port's setting was its own: a note travel time of 0.4-3.0 s in 0.1 s steps, shown in seconds (M7, `202611031153-analysis.md`), applied to every chart.

**The game:**
- **Text** (`message.pac`, messages 711, 724-732): "**Rhythm Icon Speed**", with five levels: **Slow, A Bit Slow, Normal, A Bit Fast, Fast**. Messages 724-726 name difficulty groups ("BEGINNER, STANDARD"; "HYPER, ADVENTURE"; "EASY, NORMAL, HARD, EXTREME, CHALLENGE"). *Hypothesis:* they label the speed setting per group, or its help text.
- **Values:** `s_CommonParam` (`live_common.pac/live_params.bin`) starts with a **9 × 5 table of travel times in ms**. 9 rows match the 9 difficulty indices of `s_live_datas` +0x14 (arcade EASY…CHALLENGE, home BEGINNER…ADVENTURE). 5 columns run Slow → Fast:

  | Row (*Hypothesis*: difficulty index) | Slow | A Bit Slow | **Normal** | A Bit Fast | Fast |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0 | 2300 | 2100 | **1900** | 1650 | 1300 |
  | 1 | 2000 | 1800 | **1600** | 1300 | 1000 |
  | 2 | 1700 | 1500 | **1300** | 1050 | 800 |
  | 3 | 1400 | 1200 | **1000** | 850 | 700 |
  | 4 | 1200 | 1000 | **800** | 725 | 650 |
  | 5 | 2000 | 1800 | **1600** | 1300 | 1000 |
  | 6 | 1700 | 1500 | **1300** | 1050 | 800 |
  | 7 | 1400 | 1200 | **1000** | 850 | 700 |
  | 8 | 1200 | 1000 | **800** | 725 | 650 |

**So:** in the game, harder charts move faster at the same setting, and the player picks one of five named levels. The port's fixed 1300 ms equals only row 2 at Normal. That is why the setting felt odd: it was neither the game's scale nor the game's per-difficulty speed.

**Not traced:**
- the eboot code that indexes this table;
- whether the setting is one level for all charts or one per difficulty group.

The row-per-difficulty reading fits the table's shape (9 rows) and the in-game feel (harder = faster), but needs a check against a shadPS4 frame series (note travel time on two difficulties).
