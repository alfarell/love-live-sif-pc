# M4 follow-up: port clock correction

Created: 202611010859 (Asia/Jakarta). Follows `202611010053-analysis.md` (roadmap item "Port clock: subtract the measured ~18 ms").

## Change

`AudioOutput::PlayheadMs` (`repository/src/engine/audio/audio_output.cpp`) now subtracts **two device periods** instead of one. When the SDL audio callback runs, two periods are still ahead of the system mix: the one playing and the one being filled. On this PC one period is 480 frames (10 ms in Windows shared mode), so the clock moves back by 10 ms more.

## Verification (`sif_sweep`, same method as before)

Song 0340 Easy (AC), offset 0 (presses exactly on the beat as found in the system mix), about 50 s of play:

| Clock | Mean press offset in the port | SD | Hits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before (one period) | +18.0 ms | 4.2 ms | 4 |
| **After (two periods)** | **−3.5 ms** | 5.1 ms | 33 (42 judgements, all Perfect) |

The residual −3.5 ms is within the measurement spread and well inside the ±40 ms Perfect window. Offset 0 in the port now means "on the beat as heard through the system mix". Speaker and display delay stay with `--calibrate`.

## Notes

- Verification needs a quiet system output. With a YouTube stream playing, the sweep's song match fell to 0.78 and then 0.49, and it pressed nothing. The match threshold was lowered from 0.8 to 0.7; the run above matched at 0.964.
- Only measured on this PC, with WASAPI shared mode and a 480-frame period. Other devices or exclusive mode may differ. If so, measure with `sif_sweep --keys port` again.
