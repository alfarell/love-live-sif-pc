Reading:

- `content_flags 0x0A000000` is shared by the user's P.T. PKG and the SIF PKG, and differs from the retail EU example (`0x02000000`). The user's P.T. PKG also has a `pkg_type` without the `0x80000000` bit, like SIF (`0x40000001`) and all 180 SIF DLC (`0x1`, `sif-pkg-dlc.json`).
- pt-pc says it works with CUSA01127 "as a dump folder from your console or a fake PKG made from that dump", and a store PKG "cannot be used" (`pt-pc/README.md:36-40`).
- **Hypothesis (stronger than before, still unverified):** the user's P.T. PKG and the SIF PKG are both fake PKGs rebuilt from dumps. The `0x80000000` bit in `pkg_type` and `content_flags 0x02000000` may mark store-finalized packages. The pt-pc keyset test is still the only proof, and it stays out of scope (no key material in our tools).
- PFS mode `0x0D` (signed + encrypted) is the same in all three, so the PFS mode does not tell fake from retail.
