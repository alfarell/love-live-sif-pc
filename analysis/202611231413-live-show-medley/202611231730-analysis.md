## The port: `src/app/medley_live.{h,cpp}` and `live_screen`

### `RunMedley`
`RunMedley` plays a `MedleyRun` (name, group, stage, entries). Each entry is one `RunLive` in view mode with `medley = true`:
- the list's stage;
- every member who dances the song;
- the entry's costume (`dNNN`); with a special colour, each member tries `dNNN_vC` first;
- variant 11, as View Live Show.

**Navigation:**
- A song that cannot be loaded is skipped, as the game skips a missing one.
- L1 / R1 reach the nearest song that loads. On the first / last song they do nothing (R2: R1 on the last song does nothing).
- Esc ends the medley and goes to the end screen.

**What it returns:**
- the seconds the music played;
- the entries played to their end;
- whether the last song ended.

### `RunLive` with `medley`
- **The HUD:** `uil0004_MedleyMode00` with the song name; □ (Q) hides it. There are no camera modes or photos.
- **L1 / R1** (Page Up / Down) end the song with `LiveResult::skip` −1 / +1.
- **From the second song on** (`medley_title`):
  - 5 s (the eboot's 300 frames) of the title band over the interval camera, with the music held back (silence: a song left with L1/R1 stops at once);
  - then the song's own camera and the music.
- **The interval camera:** one shot of the beach ball, chosen to look like shadPS4's frame. *Shortcut:* the same shot on every stage; the drifts are not used.

### `RunMedleyEnd`
- the group's background;
- the list's name;
- the rows "NN.Song / Singers": a whole-group song shows the group, others the members' names;
- the help line (426).

The rows match shadPS4 exactly, e.g. "02.Kimetayo Hand in Hand / Riko Sakurauchi Chika Takami You Watanabe".

**Shortcuts:**
- the 3D member on the right is not drawn (as on View Live Show's end);
- **the "What will you do next?" box is not built.** Its choices are keys for now: Enter (OPTIONS) = Watch the Same Medley List, Space (✕ End) = Return to Medley Selection, Esc = Return to Main Menu.

### Starting it (until R5)
- Top menu → Play → **Live Show Medley** (entry 3) plays `--medley ID`, else the current group's first recommended list.
- After a medley played to its end, `save::CountMedley` adds the seconds and the watched songs and costumes (log "missions: medley …"). The mission checks themselves are R6.
- "Return to Medley Selection" goes back to the top menu for now.

## A fix in View Live Show (M15)

A viewed live has no judging, but `LiveResult::finished` came from the note judge, so it was never true. As a result, **View Live Show's mission counters (`views`, `view.<song>`, `view_costumes`, `camera.<member>`) were never counted.** `finished` is now "the music ended" for a viewed live, so mission kinds 12 (View Live Shows), 26 (Member camera minutes), 36 (views of a song) and 43 (view in a costume) count as designed. Photos (kinds 13, 33, 45) were not affected: they are counted when photos are saved.
