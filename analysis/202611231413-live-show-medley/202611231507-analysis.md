## The Set List screen (`b1`, `b2`)

Play → Live Show Medley opens **"Live Show Medley: Set List Selection"** at once. There is no submenu, and no entry for others' lists.

- **The two tabs** are "Your" and "Recommended!" (L1/R1 Switch). Your tab is first.
- **On the left:** the ten lists of the current group, as memo plates.
  - Yours are "Original Set List1"-"Original Set List10".
  - The 10 recommended ones are the group's rows of `s_medley_datas`, in id order. Long names scroll.
- **On the right:** the chosen list.
  - The stage picture and name at the top left ("Sunshine Arena": stage 102, the default for Aqours).
  - The list name in pink, and the group logo.
  - 20 lines:
    - each line is "NN.Song / Singers", with a hanger icon and the costume name;
    - empty lines read "NN.(Not Set)";
    - the singers are the group ("Aqours") or the unit's members ("Riko Sakurauchi Chika Takami You Watanabe"), and a long line scrolls.
- **Hints:**
  - Your tab: L2 Toggle Mode, R2 Switch Members, □ Rename List, ↕ Select List, touchpad Start Live Show, ○ Back, ✕ Edit List;
  - Recommended tab: the same without Rename and Edit. The red note at the top left reads "You cannot edit the recommended set list." (625).
- **The help line:** 413 on Your tab, 412 on Recommended.

## The medley live (`c0`, `c1`, `next/`, `end/`)

**The HUD:**
- top right: "L1 Previous song / R1 Next song", and "□ Info ON/OFF";
- bottom right: "♪ <song title>".

There is no camera or photo hint. *Hypothesis:* the Member camera and photos are not offered in a medley.

**The songs:**
- they play on the list's one stage;
- each song brings its own members: song 2, a unit song, had only its three members;
- each wears the entry's costume ("School Unity" in all four).

**Between songs** (`next/`, R1 pressed; frames 0.6 s apart). This is about 6 s:
1. The stage fades into a **picture**: for Aqours, a beach with a beach ball. *Hypothesis:* `ui_medley_title_back00_aq` or `01_aq`.
2. A **sparkle sweeps** across, and a **band** opens:
   - a white part with the next song's title ("Kimetayo Hand in Hand");
   - a blue part with its credits ("Lyrics: … / Composition / Arrangement: …").
3. After about 4 s, the picture fades back to the stage, now with the next song's members, and the music starts.

The band is not the `medley_title_T_<song>` picture as R1 guessed. *Hypothesis:* that texture is the white part's title, or a picture for the Set List screen.

**R1 and L1:**
- R1 on the last song does nothing; L1 was not tried.
- The medley ends when the last song ends. A song skipped with R1 still seems to count as played; R1 §3 says only a finished song counts. Not checked.

## The end (`end2/`, `e0`, `e1`)

**"Live Show Medley: End the Live Show":**
- the list's name, and the songs as pink rows ("01.Song / Singers", scrolling);
- **the navigator member in 3D on the right**, in her menu costume;
- the help line "The Live Show has ended."; hints OPTIONS Display Menu, ✕ End.

**OPTIONS → "What will you do next?"** offers three choices:

- **Return to Medley Selection** (571), which goes back to Set List Selection on the Recommended tab;
- **Return to Main Menu** (566).

## Editing your list (`f3`, `g0`-`g5`)

**✕ Edit List** turns the screen into **"Live Show Medley: Set list editing"**:
- the first line is highlighted;
- hints: R1 Clear, R2 Insert, L2 Delete, □ Change Stage, ↕ Select List, touchpad Finish Editing, ○ Cancel, ✕ Modify.

**✕ Modify** opens **"Edit Slot 01"** (632):
- a "Songs" row: the song's cover, its title and a play mark;
- a "Costumes" row: the costume set's picture with a heart-lock badge, its name and a hanger;
- OK.

The empty slot came prefilled ("Kimetayo Hand in Hand", "Koini Naritai AQUARIUM"). *Hypothesis:* the last song and costume played.

**The costume row** opens a strip of costume-set cards along the bottom: "1/23 Sets", L1/R1 pages, OPTIONS Detail View, the help "Select a costume." (417).

**○** asks "Discard edits and return to list?" (637).

**□ Change Stage** opens "Change Stage" (633): one "Stage" row (picture and name) and OK; help "Confirm these choices." (418).

**Not seen:**
