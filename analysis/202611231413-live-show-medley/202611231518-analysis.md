## The data: `src/game/medley/medley.{h,cpp}`

- **`medley::Load`** reads `common/common.pac/medley.pac/medley_data.bin` → `ll3::medley::s_medley_datas`. It gives 20 `SetList`s with:
  - the id;
  - the name in four languages;
  - the group (`+0x28`);
  - the stage (`+0x2c`);
  - the entries, up to the first empty song. An entry is a song (`+0x30`), a costume (`+0x80`) and a special colour (`+0xd0`).
- **Helpers:**
  - `OfGroup(data, group)`: the 10 lists of µ's or Aqours, in id order;
  - `DefaultStage(group)`: 1 or 102;
  - `StageFolder(n)`: `stNNN`;
  - `MissionCostume(c)`: `c % 1000`, with 217 counted as 117, for kind 47;
  - `kEntries` = 20, `kOwnLists` = 10.

## The save: `src/game/save/save.{h,cpp}`

**Your own Set Lists** are `SaveData::medleys`, a map from (group, slot 0-9) to a `MedleyList`:
- a name ("" means "Original Set List<slot+1>");
- a stage (0 means the group's default);
- 20 entries; song 0 is an empty slot, so gaps are kept.

**Two save lines:**
- `medley G SLOT STAGE` followed by 20 × `song costume colour`;
- `medley_name G SLOT text`, only when the list has a name. Spaces in the name are kept.

A short line or a slot out of range is kept as an unknown line, not read. Older saves have no lists.

**The counters** are fed by `save::CountMedley(save, mode, seconds, watched)`, for a medley played to its end:

| Kind | Where it is counted |
|---|---|
| 14 / 15 | counter `gG.medley_seconds` (per group, as the eboot's `G+0x8fc6c + group × 4`) |
| 37 | set `medley_songs`: songs of finished medleys |
| 47 | set `medley_costumes`: costumes of finished medleys, as `MissionCostume` |

**Change from the game:** the game checks 37 and 47 against the medley just finished, at that moment. The port keeps every song and costume ever finished in a medley, as M15 does for View Live Show (`view_costumes`). Since mission progress is only checked when a mission is reached, this gives the same result.
