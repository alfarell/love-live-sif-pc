**Card pictures:** they come drawn with their text, one archive per language:
- big: `ui/ui_skill_card_thumbnail[_en|_cn|_tw].pac/texture.pac/skc_<id>`;

**Text boxes:** the box numbers come from the game's text table, not from the `_textNN` names. They were found by labelling every box in a test run:

- **`uip0001_SkillList00`:** 6 heading; row k: 2k name, 2k+1 level.
- **`uip0052_PopSelectListWin00`:** 0 heading, 2 "Remove".

**Messages:** 490 "(Not possessed)%s", 587-589 the three button names, 605 "Skill Card in Use", 606 "Profile Card in Use", 612 "Skill Info", 615 "[Automatic activation]", 622 "Remove", 650 "Receive Everyone's Profile Cards", 684 "Skill not set.".

## Change

**`src/app/skill_screen.h/.cpp` (new):**
- **`SkillSetup`:** the card data, the save and the played member.
- **`SkillThumbPath`:** the card picture path for a language.
- **`ApplySkillRows`:** the three "Skill Card in Use" rows of a skill list part.
- **`RunSkillSelect`:** the pop-up.
  - **Shown:** the member's owned cards in two rows, with a star on cards in use; the three slots; R1 (Page Down) Remove; OK; Skill Info for the focused card or slot; the focus frame.
  - **Setting a card:** Space on a slot goes to the list, and Space on a card puts it in that slot. A card is in at most one slot.
  - **Saving:** changes go into `save.skill_slots`.
  - **Log lines** for routes: `skill select: member 1, 4 owned cards`, `slot 2 = 11003 Finale Support`, `set 11000 11003 12016`.
- **`RunSkillCollection`:** Collection → Skill Card.
  - **Shown:** the group's member tabs (L1/R1, and Tab for L2 µ's/Aqours); all the member's cards, owned ones face up and the rest as card backs; the µ's or Aqours header; Skill Info for owned cards. The window title names an unowned card "(Not possessed)…".

**`src/app/song_select.cpp`:**
- **New step `kPrepareStep`** after the costume step, when `skills` is given (in both modes; the header atlas has the green AC row).
- **Layout:** draws `uil0027_LiveStart00` over the step layout, hides the song preview, member chibis, sort bar and page markers, and fills the profile frame (empty), the skill rows, "Receive Everyone's Profile Cards", the three buttons (icons, names, focus) and LIVE START.
- **Keys:** Left/Right move between Game Settings, Skill Card Selection and LIVE START (Profile Card Selection is skipped, as greyed in the game). Space on Skill Card Selection opens the pop-up. Enter (the Options key) or Space on LIVE START starts the live. Esc goes back to the costume step.
- **Fix:** the group header (μ's or Aqours/Saint Snow background and tab) now follows the current song. Before, it always showed Aqours.

**`src/app/main.cpp`:**
- loads the card data;
- Collection entry 17 opens `RunSkillCollection`;
- the song select gets the skill setup;
- the save is written after the select, since the slots may have changed.
- **Played member:** `--member`, else member 1. *Shortcut:* the port's navigator is always Honoka, so the played member is too.

**Routes:**
