## What the first run found

The first full run passed 16 of 21. The five failures had four causes:

1. **A real bug in the song select.** Esc was meant to go back one step (PLAY.md), but a misplaced `else` made almost every Esc leave to the top menu:

   ```cpp
   if (step != kSongStep) step = Step(step - 1);
   if (step == kStageStep && stages.empty()) step = kDifficultyStep;
   else fade.Leave(ui);
   ```

   **Fixed** in `src/app/song_select.cpp`: Esc at the song list leaves; any other Esc goes back one step.
2. **A gap in the route player.** Calibration ends the app by itself right after logging its result, and the route's `expect log` was never reached. **Fixed** in `src/app/route.cpp` `ExitCode`: when the app ends, the remaining `expect log`/`nolog` checks are still decided; waits and shots are skipped; any key or screen check left fails as before.
3. **A wrong expectation:** the volume step is 0.05, so three presses give 0.15, not 0.3. The two options routes were corrected.
4. **The costumes route reached owned costumes.** The user's own add-ons (found next to the dump) own most costumes, so Enter put them on instead of making one. The route now walks 40 cells and presses Enter twice on each.
