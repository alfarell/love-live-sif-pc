## The layouts

`ui2/20_setup` holds each difficulty layout twice:

| Layout | Home ("OR", original) | Arcade ("AC") |
|---|---|---|
| Song-step cards (`uip0011_MusicDifficulty00`, `common_uip0010_DifficultyStar00`, `uip0020_Clear_status00`) | `uil0011_MusicDiff_OR00`, 4 entries | `uil0012_MusicDiff_AC00`, 5 entries |

The port hid the home forms until now. They use the same parts, so the home mode is a switch of layout names and indices, not new drawing.

**The header** (`uip0002_StepTop00`):
- `title_mode00` (`setup_title_mode00_T`, rows of 1/4): "Play Live Show:" (pink), "Play Live Show(AC):" (green), "View Live Show:", "Live Show Medley:";

The port drew the pink "Play Live Show:" in the arcade mode as well. The game's home select is pink (shadPS4, M10). The arcade mode now shows its green title and green page names. *Hypothesis:* green pages for AC; the game's AC select was not captured in this milestone.

**Locks** (`uip0030_DifficultySelect00`):
- `select_playstatus_tex00` (`setup_select_playstatus_text00_01_T`): "Unlock by getting A or higher in HYPER";
- `select_playstatus_tex01` (`_02_T`): "Unlock by getting A or higher in EXTREME(AC)";

- the song-step card has its own `lock_icon00`.

So the game also locks the arcade CHALLENGE chart.

## Change

**`src/app/song_select.cpp`**, a `Mode` (home or arcade). It picks:
- the number of difficulties (4 or 5);
- the variant (10 + d or d);
- the difficulty index into `SongInfo::stars` (5 + d or d);
- the label cell (d or 4 + d);
- the layouts; the other mode's layouts are hidden.

**Other song select changes:**
- **Header:** the mode row and the page half as above.
- **First focus:** STANDARD in the home mode, as in the game. The arcade mode keeps CHALLENGE. Coming back from a live keeps the last difficulty when it belongs to the same mode.
- **Lock:** `Locked()` locks the home ADVENTURE card until the save holds rank A or better (best_rank ≥ 3) on that song's HYPER (variant 12). A locked plate shows the padlock and the hint instead of "Unplayed". The song-step card shows its padlock. Enter on it logs `song select: difficulty 3 is locked` and stays.
  - *shortcut:* the arcade CHALLENGE lock is not applied, so the arcade mode, its routes and the user's play stay as before.
- **Records:** looked up by the mode's variant.

**`song_select.h`:** `RunSongSelect(..., bool home)`. **`main.cpp`:** top-menu entry 0 ("Play Live Show") opens the home mode; any other entry opens the arcade one. The mode is kept while going back and forth between the select and lives.

**Routes:**
- `career_1_play`, `career_2_play_again`, `play_keys` and `select_back` used Enter on the menu's first entry. They now press Down to "Play Live Show (AC)" and check the log for it, so they keep testing the arcade mode. `career_1_play` expects `top menu: entry 1`.
- **New `home_select`:**
  - opens entry 0 and checks that the select is in the home mode;
  - shoots the difficulty step;
  - checks that ADVENTURE is locked;
  - goes back to HYPER, starts it (`variant 12`, difficulty index 7), shoots the live and returns with Esc.
- **New `home_unlocked`:** uses the seed `seeds/home_unlocked.txt` (song 0010 HYPER at rank A, port-made). ADVENTURE opens, and its live starts (`variant 13`, index 8).
