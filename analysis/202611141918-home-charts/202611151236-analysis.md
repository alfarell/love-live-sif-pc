## Change (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`, `Playfield`)

**`SetHome(chart.home)`** moves four rings onto the arcade rings' arc. The arc's centre is midway between ring01 and ring09, about (960, 150); its radius reaches down to ring05, about 826 px.

| Lane | Button | Angle | Arcade spot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | L1 | 180° | ring01, the arcade L2 spot |
| 2 | ← | 140° | none |
| 3 | ↓ | 112.5° | ring04, unchanged |
| 5 | ✕ | 67.5° | ring06, unchanged |
| 6 | ○ | 40° | none |
| 7 | R1 | 0° | ring09, the arcade R2 spot |

The first try moved only ← and ○. L1 and R1 then sat at the arcade L1/R1 spots (157.5° and 22.5°), not in the corners as in the game. The capture showed it, and it is fixed.

**`Shown(lane)`:** in a home live only the six home lanes get a ring. L2, the centre and R2 are not drawn.

**`Blue(lane)`:** L1 and R1 use the blue art the game has next to the pink and orange art:
- the blue indicator, `ui_ingame_judgering_all_mark` cell (1, 0);
- the blue note ring, `ui_ingame_notes` (3, 0);
- the dark blue fill, (3, 4).

**Arcade lives are unchanged.** `home` is false, so the rings and notes are drawn as before.
