## Change

**`src/app/input.h/.cpp`:**
- **Home mappings:** `HomeSlots` (6 buttons for lanes 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7), with defaults and text in and out (one template shared with the 10-slot arcade text).
- **Two home configs,** `HomeConfig::Easy` (BEGINNER/STANDARD) and `Hard` (HYPER/ADVENTURE).
- **`SetLiveHome(home, config)`:** picks the mapping the live's pad buttons use.

**`src/app/live_screen.cpp`:**
- sets the live mapping from the chart: variants 10/11 use Easy, 12/13 use Hard. It is cleared when the live ends;
- the rings' button icons show that mapping (`Playfield::SetHome`).

**Settings:**
- `[controls] pad_lanes_home_easy` and `pad_lanes_home_hard`, 6 names each; empty = defaults;
- `main.cpp` loads them, and warns on a bad value.

**`src/app/options_screen.cpp`:**
- **`RunControlSettings`:**
  - draws the game's Control Settings with its three rows (message texts, English fallbacks; "Modify" is the port's word);
  - Up/Down move the focus, Enter opens that row's Button Controls, and returning comes back to the list. Esc leaves.
  - *shortcut:* Vibration and OK are hidden; the port has no vibration, and Esc stands for OK.
- **`RunButtons(..., home, config)`:**
  - the 9-lane form as before, or the 6-lane form (plates and rings `_10`-`_15`, the game's heading);
  - Left/Right pick a slot, Up/Down change its button, T resets;
  - leaving saves the config's setting and logs `options: pad lanes HYPER, ADVENTURE …`.
- **Known difference:** the game shows both top rings (L1, R1) blue in the 6-lane form. The port lights only the focused slot's ring, as in its 9-lane form.

**Routes:**
- `controls` now goes Control Settings → the AC row → Button Controls → back to the list → back to the menu. It adds a shot `control_settings`.
- **New `controls_home`:** the HYPER/ADVENTURE row; L1 → L2 in the first slot; the log shows `L2 LEFT DOWN CROSS CIRCLE R1`, and so does `settings.ini` (`pad_lanes_home_hard`), while `pad_lanes_home_easy` stays empty.
