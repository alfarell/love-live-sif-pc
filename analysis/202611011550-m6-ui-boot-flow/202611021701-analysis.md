# M6: ring break on a Miss

Created: 202611021701 (Asia/Jakarta). Follows `202611021656-analysis.md`.

## 1. `ui_ingame_judgering_break.fad`

- 60 frames (1 s) on the usual 400 × 400 canvas.
- Textures: `ui_ingame_kira_03`, `ui_ingame_common_circle_05`, `ui_ingame_judgering_break_01` and `_02` (100 × 100 shards), and others in its texture list. Its sprites carry their own scales (0.2–0.96) inside the sprite definitions; the decoder already applies them.
- The pieces gather around the canvas centre, at about lane-ring size. `ui_ingame_judgering_exact` (192 px) is a ring the size of the drawn lane ring.

## 2. Port (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`)

`Playfield` loads it with `LoadFad`. A **Miss** on a lane restarts it, centred on that lane's ring. The trigger is a guess from the name and the ring-sized shards (marked as a `shortcut:` in the code); the game's use is not traced.

**Check:** song 0010 played without input (every note missed), captures at 14, 17.5 and 21 s (`work/m6/break_sheet.png`, `break_zoom.png`):
- the MISS text shows;
- a white flash with shards sits on the third lane's ring after a miss there.

Debug and Release are rebuilt, and all 7 tests pass.
