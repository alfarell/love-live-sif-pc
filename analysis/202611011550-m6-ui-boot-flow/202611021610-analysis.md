## 2. Port (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`, `Playfield`)

- `LoadJudgeTexts` loads the five textures and one full-texture quad.
- `DrawJudgeText` draws the latest judgement's word at its texture size, centred at canvas (960, 350), just above the combo counter. It pops in (scale 1.25 → 1 over 100 ms), holds, and fades over its last 200 ms of the 1050 ms suggested by the `.fad` header. A new judgement replaces it.
