# M6: the game's play field and HUD bar in the live screen

Created: 202611020049 (Asia/Jakarta). Follows `202611020043-analysis.md`.

## 1. Two in-game UI sets

| Set | Content |
|---|---|
| `ui/ui_ingame.pac` (loaded by name in the eboot's package table) | The play field. `layout.pac` holds two `BLBN` tables. `anim.pac` holds 9 `.fad` animations (judgement texts PERFECT/GREAT/GOOD/BAD/MISS and "support" variants, ring break, LIVE CLEAR); format not decoded. `texture.pac` holds 60 textures: note atlas, circles, combo digits, judgement texts, Perfect effects, long-note circle, result backgrounds |
| `ui2/90_ingame[_<lang>]` (11 layouts, 46 parts) | The HUD bar `uil0001_Ingame00`: rank gauge, FULL COMBO flower marks, score digits, song gauge, song name. Also the Live View camera info (`uil0002`), the song title intro (`uil0003`), medley info, active skills and the pre-live skill list |

### `ui_ingame_layout.bin` (`BLBN` `layout_datas`)

32-byte records: u32, u32, u64 name\*, f32 rect (x0, y0, x1, y1) in 1920 × 1080 pixels, y downwards.

| Name | Centre (px) | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `layout_judge_ring01` … `09` | (135, 150), (198, 466), (377, 734), (645, 913), (960, 976), (1275, 913), (1543, 734), (1722, 466), (1785, 150) | 152 |
| `layout_center_circle` | (958, 146) | 288 |
| `layout_FULLCOMBO` | (962, 500) | 1052 × 147 |

`ui_ingame_layout_up.bin` (45 records) places the title bar, player frame, member icons, skill icons, "kizuna" gauge, credit text and so on (*Hypothesis:* the arcade cabinet's or an alternative "up" layout).

### `ui_ingame_notes`

A 1280 × 1536 atlas of 256-pixel cells (5 × 6):
- **row 0:** ERROR placeholder, pink ring, pink disc, blue ring, stars;
- **row 1:** yellow note faces (star, anchor, snowman) and a pink glow;
- **row 2:** small icons;
- **row 3:** long-note bars and end caps (orange, blue);
- **rows 4–5:** attribute icons (mic, face, dress) with trails, and blue discs.

## 2. Port (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`)

- `Playfield` reads the layout table and the atlas (plus the centre-circle gauge and microphone textures) and draws sprites on the canvas:
  - pink ring cell at the 9 ring positions;
  - the centre circle;
  - notes as the yellow star cell, flying from the centre circle and growing to the ring;
  - hold trails as pink-disc cells;
  - the judgement flash as a tinted disc.
- If the play field cannot be loaded, the old placeholder lanes are drawn.
- The HUD bar (`uil0001_Ingame00`) is drawn over the scene through `UiScreen`:
  - effect instances (`FFmark_ef`, `FinaleON00`) are hidden;
  - the song-name text box (part 5, box 1) is set from `s_music_datas` in the UI language (`LiveOptions::lang`, from `--lang`).
- **Check:**
  - `work/m6/ingame_a.png`: song 0010, rings, centre circle, star notes.
  - `work/m6/ingame_b.png`: song 0020 with the HUD bar (RANK gauge with C/B/A/S, star row, `score 0000000`, the bottom song bar).
- Frame cost (Debug, 9 members): 15.5 ms mean CPU, unchanged from M5.
- All 5 tests pass.

## 3. Shortcuts and open

1. **Atlas cells** for buttons, notes and holds are picked by eye. The game's note drawing (which cell for which note type, attribute colours, long-note bars) is not traced, and the `.fad` animation format is not decoded, so there are no PERFECT/GREAT texts and no ring break.
2. **HUD values:** score digits, rank gauge, song gauge and FULL COMBO marks stay at their defaults; the song name does not show yet (box placement or style to check).
3. **Combo counter** (`ui_ingame_combo_num`) not drawn.
4. Intro title (`uil0003_LiveTitle00`), skills and Live View mode not used.
