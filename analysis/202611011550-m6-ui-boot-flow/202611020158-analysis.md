# M6: song progress gauge (centre circle)

Created: 202611020158 (Asia/Jakarta). Follows `202611020135-analysis.md`.

## 1. The part

`ui2/90_ingame` part `uip0006_Songgage` sits in the HUD layout `uil0001_Ingame00`, on the centre circle. It has three 188 × 188 quads, each with its own 188 px texture:

| Node | Texture |
|---|---|
| `IngamesonggageBase` | pale double ring with a glow: the empty track |
| `IngamesonggageCol` | solid pink ring: the filled part |
| `IngamesonggageMike` | microphone icon |

The part has only a loop clip (`a003`) and no track that could fill the ring. The fill must therefore be drawn by code (*Hypothesis*; not traced). A plain UV offset cannot do it, because the fill grows around the circle rather than sideways.

The play field's `ui/ui_ingame.pac` texture `ui_ingame_centercircle_gauge_01_aq` (212 px) is the same pink ring. Drawn at the layout's centre-circle size (288 px), it was larger than the HUD track.

## 2. Port (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`, `Playfield`)

- The gauge is a dynamic triangle-fan mesh: one centre vertex and 65 points on the inscribed circle. Its UVs equal its positions, so it shows the texture unchanged and only clips its angle.
- `SetGauge(progress)` rewrites the vertices outside the frame when progress moves by 1/2048 or more. Progress = audio playhead / audio length.
- The texture is `IngamesonggageCol` from `ui2/90_ingame.pac`, drawn at 188 px. The HUD's own `IngamesonggageCol` node is hidden, so the HUD base shows the empty part.
- Fill direction: clockwise from 12 o'clock (*Hypothesis*; a `shortcut:` comment in the code).

**Check:** `work/m6/gauge_b.png` and its 3× crop `gauge_b_zoom.png` were taken about 38 s into song 0010 (126 s) on autoplay. The deep pink arc runs from 12 o'clock to about 4 o'clock, roughly 0.3 of a turn, over the pale track. All 5 tests pass.
