  - five arcade cards (`uil0012_MusicDiff_AC00`): label by UV cell; level badge cell = level − 1;
  - rank, lock and clear status hidden (no save);
  - a card with no chart is hidden.
- **Difficulty step:** each plate (`uip0030`) gets its label (`M_difficulty_level00_big`).
- **Selection panel (`uip0004_SelectList00`):**
  - the song's cover;
  - the selected difficulty's small label and 10 stars;

  - the panel uses its duo group for 2 members, its 8-slot group for even counts and its 11-slot group for odd counts, centred (*Hypothesis*, marked as a `shortcut:`).
