# M6: result rank, clear rate and FULL COMBO

Created: 202611020222 (Asia/Jakarta). Follows `202611020207-analysis.md`.

## 1. Rank letters (confirms the U5 hypothesis)

`ui2/95_result` part `uip0005_ScoreRate00` (in `uil0007_ScoreRateGroup00`) has two meshes:
- `score_rate00` (material `score_rate_M`);
- `score_rate00_eff` (material `score_rate_eff_M`).

Both show the cell U 0–0.25, V 0–0.5 of `res_result_evaluation00` (1440 × 512). The atlas holds **D C B A** on the top row and **S SS SSS** below. That gives seven ranks in threshold order, matching the seven ranks from `s_CommonParam` +0x138…+0x14C found in U5. The letter names are no longer a hypothesis.

Rank k is drawn by shifting both materials by U = 0.25 × (k mod 4), V = 0.5 × ⌊k / 4⌋.

## 2. Clear-rate number

`ui_number.bin` gives `uip0003_PlayScore00` three right-aligned 6-digit boxes:

| Box | Digit set | Default |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (score) | 0 | `999999` |
| 1 (max combo) | 1 | `123456` |
| 2 (clear rate) | 2 | `100000` |

Digit set 2 is the green row of `res_record_num00` (640 × 256, 64 px cells). Its glyph slots 10–19 continue on the next texture row. Slot 15 is **%** and slot 18 is **.**; their advances in `s_ui_numtex_datas` (35) equal the digits'. `UiScreen::DrawNumbers` now maps '%' and '.' to those slots.

The port shows the rate as `%.2f%%`, rounded down (e.g. `100.00%`). The two-decimal format is a *Hypothesis*: the 6-digit default could also be 4 decimals without a point.
