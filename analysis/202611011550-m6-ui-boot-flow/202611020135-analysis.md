# M6: live combo counter

Created: 202611020135 (Asia/Jakarta). Follows `202611020132-analysis.md`.

## 1. Asset

`ui/ui_ingame.pac/texture.pac/ui_ingame_combo_num` (840 × 200, RGBA):
- digits 0–9 on the top row, in 84 × 100 cells. The glyph bounding boxes, measured from the alpha channel, lie within x 3–84 and y 8–93 of each cell.
- the "COMBO" label below, at about (3, 139)–(182, 192).

`ui_ingame_layout.bin` has no combo entry; the only related record is `layout_FULLCOMBO`. The combo's position and spacing therefore come from the game's code, which is not traced.

## 2. Port (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`, `Playfield`)

- `Quad` builds a unit quad for any UV rectangle (`Cell` now uses it).
- `LoadCombo` makes one mesh per digit cell plus one for the label.
- `DrawCombo` draws the count centred at canvas (960, 470), just below the centre circle, with the label under it.
- Guesses (*Hypothesis*), marked as a `shortcut:` in the code:
  - the position;
  - the digit advance (0.86 of a cell);
  - showing the count from a combo of 2.

**Check:** `work/m6/combo_a.png` was taken about 20 s into song 0010 on autoplay. It shows "50 COMBO" under the centre circle, and the HUD score reads 0018690. All 5 tests pass.
