## 2. Port (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`)

- **Simultaneous notes:** consecutive chart notes with the same time (within 1 ms) are joined by the orange bar, stretched and rotated between their current positions. It is drawn under the notes, at a thickness of 32/256 of the note size. It is not drawn once either note is held or judged.
- **Hold glow:** while a long note is held, `ui_ingame_longnote_circle_01` is drawn over it on its lane ring, at 1.58 × ring size (matching the ring), pulsing.
- Both rules are guesses from how SIF looks (marked as a `shortcut:` in the code); the game's note drawing is not traced.
