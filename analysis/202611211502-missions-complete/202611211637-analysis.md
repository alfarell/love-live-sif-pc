## How each kind is counted

**The group rows (p1 = the group, 0 µ's, 1 Aqours)** read the Q3 per-group counters `gG.*`. This follows Q2: µ's GREAT 76/100 against Aqours 0/100.
- 0: plays; 1: a FULL COMBO; 2: best rank ≥ S; 3: best rank SSS (stored as rank + 1);
- 4: best score; 5: GREATs; 6: PERFECTs.
- 7: owned costumes of the group. A recipe's group comes from its id range; Saint Snow counts with Aqours.
- 11: every key item of the group (item type 1 µ's, 2 Aqours) in the `keys` set.

**Totals:** 8/9/10 `collected.key/thread/cloth`; 12 `views`; 13 `photos`.
- *Shortcut:* 8-10 are counted for both groups together, since the counters are not split per group.

**Member rows (p1 = the member):**
- 20: lives with her (`bonds[p1].lives`, from M14);
- 21: phrases heard (`talk.M`);
- 22: skill card types owned; 23: all of them (need = her card count); 31: cards at max level.
  - *Shortcut:* "normal" cards in 31 are taken to be those that level (`max_level > 1`).
- 24: profile cards made (`cards.M`);
- 26: minutes in the Member camera (`camera.M` / 60);
- 27: costumes she wore on the menu (`menu.M`); 28: all of them (need = the costumes of her group);
- 29: owned accolades among the ones her member missions (kinds 20-34 with p1 = her) give.
  - *Hypothesis:* "her accolades" means those rewards.
- 33: a Member camera photo of her (`camera_photo`).

**Song and costume rows:** 36 `view.S`; 40 costume owned (`costume::Owns`); 42 the costume in any `menu.M`; 43 `view_costumes`; 45 `photo.D`; 46 `card_costumes`; 48 `special`.
