## The data

- **`mission::Condition`** now keeps its two parameter types, `p1_type` and `p2_type` (bytes `+0x41` and `+0x42`). The unit test reads them from the real table:
  - kind 0: a group and a count;
  - kind 20: a member;
  - kind 35: a song and a count;
  - kind 40: a costume.
- **`Mission::Rule()`** is the show rule (row byte `+5`, the code's second byte).

## Which rows are listed

**`mission::Fits(m, c, member, song_mode)`** is the eboot's `FUN_001c6bd0`. It checks each parameter by its type:
- **group:** the current member's mode;
- **member:** her;
- **song:** her mode's songs;
- **costume:** her group's costumes.
  - *Shortcut:* the costume's group comes from its id range (µ's 0-99, Aqours 100-199, Saint Snow 200+). The eboot reads the costume's group mask `+0x54`.

**`mission::Shown(m, all, save, owned)`** is `FUN_001c6ea0`:
- **rule 0:** listed, but once claimed it is hidden when the next row is its next step;
- **rule 1:** listed after the previous step is claimed;
- **rules 2 and 3:** listed when the song or costume is owned (costume 217 is checked as 117).

The add-on filter from before stays: songs and costumes the user has no add-on for are left out.

**Effect in the career route** (Honoka, then Chika):
- before: 1464 member, 200 group, 1625 song, 769 costume rows;
- now (Honoka): 13 member, 18 group, 360 song, 293 costume rows;
- now (Chika): 13 member, 18 group, 265 song, 174 costume rows.
