Date: 2026-11-07 20:26 (Asia/Jakarta). Steps 5 and 6 of `plan/202611021940-m7-systems-breadth/202611071847-plan.md`.

## Step 5: "badge levels" do not exist; reward type 3 is a badge

These facts come from all 229 `s_badge_datas` rows (`work/m7/tables/badge_datas.bin`, read with a probe script):

- **No levels.** A category (+0x04) is a theme with 1–11 named badges (e.g. 65: 焔の番人, 野生のツチノコ探し隊 …; 18: 穂乃果推し！, おひさま). All badges of a category share its one picture (`badge_plate_NNN_0`, no other suffix in the dump). The roadmap's "badge levels" was a misreading, so there is nothing to build.
- **Starters.** +0x18 is 256 only for 901001–903001 (µ's, Aqours, Saint Snow 大好き). That matches the three badges a new save owns on the game's Profile Card screen, so the port's starter set is now backed by the table.
- **+0x14:** *Hypothesis*, the group the badge belongs to (1 µ's, 2 Aqours, 7 on the Saint Snow-themed category 5 …), not a bit mask as first written.
- **Reward type 3:** every one of its 120 mission rows gives id 5003, the badge "どさんこ" (category 5). So type 3 is a badge reward, not a booster. Claimed type-3 rewards are now choosable on the Profile Card screen (`MakeNameplate`). `mission.md`, `mission.h` and `save.h` say so.

## Step 6: the 3- and 6-lane Button Controls are out of M7

- The game's Control Settings has three configs (202611071821): the 9-lane AC one (built), "HYPER, ADVENTURE" (6 lanes) and "BEGINNER, STANDARD".
- Those two are for the home-version charts (variants 10–13). The port does not play them:
  - their notes use note number 0, which the chart reader counts as `skipped` (`src/game/live/chart.h`; meaning unknown since M4);
  - the song select offers only the five AC difficulties (`kDifficulties`).
- So the 3- and 6-lane controls have nothing to drive. They move with the home charts to a later milestone (roadmap).
