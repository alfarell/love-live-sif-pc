## The game's own screens (seen in shadPS4)

The game ran in shadPS4 from the isolated `work/shadps4-run` folder. Keys were sent only to its window with PostMessage, and pictures were taken only of that window with PrintWindow (scratchpad `shadpost.ps1`). The route was top menu → Options → Control Settings. Captures are `work/shadps4-run/kc-15.png` (Control Settings), `kc-16.png` (AC Button Controls) and `work/m7/kc_other.png` (6-lane).

- **Control Settings** (`ui2/60_option` `uil0010_controllerOpt_layout00`), under the heading "Live Show Player Controller Settings":
  - Play Live Show: "BEGINNER, STANDARD" and "HYPER, ADVENTURE", each with a Modify button.
  - Play Live Show (AC): "EASY, NORMAL, HARD, EXTREME, CHALLENGE", with a Modify button.
  - Vibration ON/OFF.
  - OK.
- **Button Controls** (`uil0011_keyConfigLayout00` > `uil0012_keySetting00`): a half ring of lane circles with a button plate beside each, plus Reset and OK.
  - The AC form, the layout's "button_9" group, has 10 plates (`uip0031_controllButton00_00-09`) and 9 rings (`uip0032_controllRing00_00-08`). The centre lane has two plates.
  - **Defaults, left to right: L2, L1, ←, ↓, → and □ (centre lane), ✕, ○, R1, R2.** ↑ and △ are not used.
  - The 6-lane form ("button_6", `_10-_15`) defaults to L1, ←, ↓, ✕, ○, R1.
- The plate icon is a 1/4 × 1/4 cell of `opt_ope_ingame_button00`. The atlas order is ↑ ← ↓ → △ □ ✕ ○, then L1 L2 R1 R2.
- **Eboot:**
  - At file offset 0x606664 there is the plain SCE pad-bit list (Select, L3, R3, Options, Up, Right, Down, Left, L2, R2, L1, R1, △, ○, ✕, □).
  - At 0x9290f0 there is a pair table that looks like a library remap, not lane defaults.
  - The default lane table itself was not found statically. The defaults above are read off the game's screen.

## What the port does now

- `src/app/input.{h,cpp}`:
  - `PadButton` (atlas order).
  - `PadSlots`: 10 slots, with `kLaneOfSlot` mapping slots 4 and 5 to the centre lane.
  - `DefaultPadSlots()` (the game's defaults).
  - Text form for settings, and `SetPadSlots`.
  - In a live, a pad button gives the lane key of the first slot that holds it.
  - L2 and R2 are analog triggers in SDL. They count as pressed past half travel and become key events.
  - The old placeholder live layout is gone.
- Settings: `[controls] pad_lanes = "L2 L1 LEFT …"`. Empty means the defaults. A bad value logs a warning and the defaults are used.
- Options → Control Settings (top-menu entry 12) opens the 9-lane Button Controls directly:
  - Left/Right pick a slot.
  - Up/Down change its button.
  - T (pad △) resets to the defaults.
  - Enter or Esc leaves.
  - The focused slot's lane ring is shown in blue, the way the game marks it.

  *Shortcuts:* the Control Settings list (the three Modify rows, Vibration) is skipped, and the 3- and 6-lane forms are not built. The port plays the AC charts. Reset and OK are keys, not buttons. A button may sit in two slots; it then plays the first one's lane, because the game's own rule for duplicates is not traced.
- Keyboard lane keys (A S D F Space J K L ;) are unchanged and not configurable.
