# M7 step: awards (accolades and badges)

## 1. Findings

Format notes are in `repository/docs/formats/mission.md`, section "Awards".

- **Accolades** (`honorific_title.pac`): 1665 rows. Mission reward type 4 is an accolade id: 1642 of 1651 type-4 rows match one.
- **Badges** (`badge.pac`): 229 rows, each with a category, an ARGB colour and a name.
- **Names:** Japanese only. Each id has one text symbol, and there are no language packages in the dump. *Hypothesis:* the PS4 game shows them in Japanese in every UI language. Not checked in the game.
- **The twin mission tracks give different accolades.** Mission 16 gives 3007 「ライブハウスの常連さん」 and its twin 103 gives 503007 「ライブ会場の常連さん」. This supports the earlier guess that rows 0-86 and 87-199 are two separate tracks (µ's and Aqours). Not yet traced in the eboot.
