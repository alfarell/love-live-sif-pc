# M7 finish, step 1: rewards and inventory

Date: 2026-11-07 18:53 (Asia/Jakarta). Step 1 of `plan/202611021940-m7-systems-breadth/202611071847-plan.md`. That plan holds the reward-type table and the item and recipe layouts, all probed with `work/m7/*.py`.

## What changed

- **Save** (`save.txt`):
  - `claimed <id> <id> …`: the missions whose reward was taken;
  - `own <type> <id> <count>`: what the player owns, keyed by mission reward type (2 item, 3 booster, 4 accolade or badge, 5 frame, 6 name plate, 7 costume, 1 card);
  - `name_plate <id>`.

  Older saves load with nothing claimed.
- **Missions:** `mission::Claim(m, save)` gives a completed, unclaimed mission's reward (its amount, at least 1) and marks it claimed. `mission::Owned(save, type)` lists the owned ids of one type. `EarnedAwards` is gone: what the player has is now what they claimed, as in the game.
- **Task List:**
  - Enter (pad Cross) on a completed mission claims it and logs `missions: claimed <id>: reward type T id I xN`.
  - The plate shows GET! (`status_icon01`) while claimable and COMPLETED (`status_icon00`) once claimed.
  - Each tab's box 0 shows how many rewards wait. Box 1 sits under it and showed the number a second time, so it is left empty.
  - The save is written when the screen closes.
- **Nameplate:**
  - Accolades and badges come from the claimed type-4 rewards (plus the starters).
  - Name plates are new: the layout's own `name_plate_011_0011` plus the claimed type-6 plates, cycled with PageUp/PageDown (pad L1/R1) in the header editor.

  *Shortcut:* the default plate's picture is only in the screen packages (`ui2/03_card.pac/texture.pac/name_plate_011_0011.btx`, also in 40_collection and 50_event), not in `ui/ui_name_plate.pac`, so it is loaded from 03_card by a special case.
