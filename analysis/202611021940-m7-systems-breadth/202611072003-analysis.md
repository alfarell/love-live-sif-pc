## What the dump has

`chr/` holds one folder per member with that member's costume models (`work/pac-list.csv`):
- µ's: `c01`–`c09`, about 36 costumes each;
- Aqours: `c11`–`c19`, d0xx–d1xx, about 34 each;
- Saint Snow: `c21`–`c22`, `d200`, `d217`, `d260`, `d999`.

So the costumes a member can wear are those with a `chr/cNN/dNNN/dNNN.pac`.

## What changed

- **Save:** `wear <member> <costume>` lines (`SaveData::wear`).
- **Create/Change Costumes:**
  - PageUp/PageDown (pad L1/R1) now switch the member being dressed, through 1–9, 11–19 and 21–22. The grid follows that member's group and starts on the costume they wear.
  - Enter on an owned costume puts it on the member if they have the model.
  - The info window adds "Member N wears: <costume>" and a hint.

  *Shortcut:* members are shown by number, because no member-name table is found yet. Group switching by itself (the game's L2) is folded into member switching.
- **Live:**
  - `LiveOptions::wear` (member → `dNNN`): a member's own costume wins over the song select's choice when the member has that model; otherwise the choice; otherwise their base model. The log says `live: member N wears dNNN (own costume)`.
  - The member loop now covers 1–22, not 1–9. Before, an Aqours or Saint Snow song loaded no dancers unless `--member` was given (*observed in the code; not re-checked on an Aqours song*).
