# M7 step: missions (tables, progress from the save)

## 1. Findings

Format notes are in `repository/docs/formats/mission.md`.

- **Where:** `common/common.pac/mission.pac` has 5 BLBN tables:
  - 4 tables of mission rows (4058 rows, 28 bytes each: id, code, p1, p2, reward type, reward id, amount);
  - 1 table of condition kinds (49 kinds, each with a title and a description in 4 languages and `[p1]` / `[p2]` placeholders).
- **Condition kind:** the low byte of the row's code. The tables split by kind:
  - common: general, kinds 0-19;
  - music: per song, kinds 35-39;
  - dress: per costume, kinds 40-48;
  - recommended: per member, kinds 20-34.
- **Related tables, not read yet:**
  - `badge.pac/badge_datas.bin` (symbols `s_text_NNNN`, badge names);
  - `honorific_title.pac/honorific_title_datas.bin` (accolades);
  - `game.pac/level_datas.bin`.
  - The eboot names more: `s_badge_datas`, `s_level_datas`, `s_experience_datas`, `s_yellpoint_datas`, `s_emblemrank_datas` (`work/eboot-strings.txt`).
- ***Hypothesis:*** the rewards are badges and accolades. Common rows 0-86 (p1 = 0) give 71xxxx and rows 87-199 (p1 = 1) give 72xxxx, so p1 there may be the group (µ's or Aqours).

## 2. Port

- `src/game/mission/mission.{h,cpp}`:
  - `Load(vfs, missions)`;
  - `ProgressOf(mission, save)` gives have / need;
  - `Fill(pattern, mission, p1_name)`.
- **Tracked from the existing save:**
  - plays (kinds 0 and 35);
  - FULL COMBO (1 and 38);
  - rank S or better and rank SSS (2 and 3);
  - best score (4 and 39).
  - The other kinds report "not tracked" (have = -1).
  - The save format is unchanged: these missions are worked out from the per-chart records.
- **Boot flow (`main.cpp`):** after each finished live it logs the missions that this live completed, with the English (or UI-language) title and text. Song titles fill `[p1]`.
- **`tests/unit/mission_test.cpp`** (`sif_mission_test <dump>`) checks:
  - the row and kind counts;
  - two English texts;
  - mission 2000 (play song 0010 once) and mission 1 (10 plays);
  - `[p1]`/`[p2]` filling;
  - progress on an empty save and after one play.
- **Verified:**
