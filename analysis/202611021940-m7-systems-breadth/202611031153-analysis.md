## 2. Port

- **`src/app/options_screen.{h,cpp}`**, `RunOptions(…, OptionsPage)`:
  - **Sound page:** one "Volume" row (`audio.volume`, steps of 5 %, applied at once through the new `AudioOutput::SetVolume`).
  - **Game Settings page:** "Note speed" (new `settings.play.approach_ms`, 0.4–3.0 s, steps of 0.1 s; used as `LiveOptions::approach_ms` at start and after the options) and "Timing offset" (`timing.audio_offset_ms`, ±300 ms, steps of 5 ms; the calibration value).
  - Other rows and the buttons are hidden. Labels are the port's own English words (`shortcut:`, until `message.pac`).
- **Boot flow:** Options → Sound or Game Settings opens the page; leaving writes `settings.ini` and returns to the top menu. The other Options entries still lead to the song select.
- **Engine:** `SceneModel::TextBoxes` skips mask nodes named `*stencil*`. Before, the gauge's untextured `volume_gauge00_Stencil` counted as text box 0 and shifted the part's real boxes. The result screen was rechecked and is unchanged (`work/m7/recheck_result2.png`).
