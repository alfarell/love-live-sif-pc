## What the port does now

- **`costume` module:** items, recipes (with descriptions), song drops, `Owns`, `CanCraft`, `Craft`, `RollDrops`, `IconPath`, `ThumbPath`.
  - **Owned costumes:** d000; the ones owned from the start (flag low byte 0); rewarded or made (`own 7 <id>`); or given by an installed add-on (`dlc::Ownership::addon_costumes`, new).
- **Drops after a live:** every finished live rolls the song's 18 drops and logs them, e.g. `materials: Black thread x10, White cloth x4, …`.
  - *Hypothesis:* a flat chance per drop; the game's bonuses are not traced.
- **Costume Materials** (`RunMaterials`, top menu Costumes → Costume Materials): the game's `uil0030_MaterialListLayout00`.
  - The 34 slots are filled in the game's order: icon (`item_icon00_M`), name (box 0), "×N" (box 1).
  - Left/Right switch µ's and Aqours.
- **Create/Change Costumes** (`RunCostumes`, top menu Costumes → Create/Change Costumes): the game's `uil0001_CosLayout00`.
  - The group's grid (`uil0003/4/5_CosMenu00_*`) with 33 slots; PageUp/PageDown switch the group.
  - Name, preview, description, and the status label (`status_label00-03` = Ready to Create, Obtained, Insufficient Materials, download only; read from the label textures).
  - Enter opens Confirm Costume Creation: up to 12 materials as need/have numbers (`uip0011_MaterialParts02`), and the Back and Create buttons (cells of `com_popup_button_text00_T`, 2 columns × rows of 1/8). Enter there makes the costume.
  - *Shortcuts:*
    - The grid and preview show the costume thumbnail instead of the member card; the card ↔ costume link is not decoded.
    - Not built: the 3D member behind the curtain, special colours, the material gauges, the focus frame, the owned check mark, the key-material song list and "Buy Costume".
- **Song select:** the costume step lists only owned costumes (`costume::Owns`).
