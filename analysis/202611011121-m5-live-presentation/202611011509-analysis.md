# M5 step 3: when the dance starts relative to the music

Created: 202611011509 (Asia/Jakarta). Follows `202611011216-analysis.md`. Plan: `plan/202611011117-m5-live-presentation/202611011117-plan.md` (step 3).

## 1. Result

**dance frame = audio time (s) × 60 + `s_music_datas[song] +0x104`**

- `s_music_datas` is in `common/common.pac/live_common.pac/live_datas.bin`: 125 records of 832 bytes, keyed by song id at +0x00.
- The field is an f32: 972.0 for song 0010 and 864.0 for song 0020. It is the frame of the song's 60 fps dance and camera clock at which the music starts.
- The frames before it are a stage intro: 16.2 s for 0010, 14.4 s for 0020.
- *Status:* strongly supported by the data below; the eboot code that reads the field is not traced (labelled as a hypothesis in the code comment).

## 3. Evidence for the rule

1. **The value is in the timeline.** Each song's camera timeline (`scene_NNNN_000_default.btlb`, a `BTL` file of frame-keyed events) has an event at exactly that frame: (972, 10, 135) for 0010, and (864, 0, 380) and (864, 1, 400) for 0020. `scene_stage_0020_00.btlb` also has 864.
2. **Song 0010:**
   - The last chart note (audio 119.86 s) lands at frame 8,164.
   - The dance's last movement ends at frame **8,167** (+3 frames).
   - The camera's last cut is at frame 8,138.
3. **Both songs follow the same pattern.**

   | Song | Music audible (dance frames) | Dance moves (frames) | Dance start after music start | Dance end before music fades |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 0010 | 1,178–8,239 | 1,504–8,167 | 5.4 s (intro) | 1.2 s |
   | 0020 | 1,197–7,920 | 1,424–7,817 | 3.8 s (intro) | 1.7 s |

4. **Rejected alternatives:**
   - Music-energy vs dance-activity cross-correlation gave a broad peak at 17.8 s (r = 0.30).
   - The onset vs dance-detail fit gave weak, beat-periodic peaks (best 20.7 s, r = 0.07).
   - Neither is decisive. With 20.7 s, song 0010's dance would end 5.7 s before the music, unlike song 0020.
   - Camera cuts are not on the beat grid (mean error 3.9 of 8.6 frames), so they don't fix the phase either.

Before the music, the dance has no keys until frame 536 and a small movement at 8–10 s. This matches the dense member events in scene ch1/ch2 at scene times that fall in the intro.
