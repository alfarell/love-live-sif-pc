## Findings

**The card part `uip0019_ProfileCardCustom00`** (mesh bounds measured from the part with `tools/bmar_dump.py --obj`):
- **The photo mesh** (`photo00` / `photo01`) is the whole 16:9 photo, 835.6 × 470.1.
- **The stencils cut a window from it:**
  - portrait: x −264.6..264.6, all of its height (u 0.183-0.817);
  - landscape: all of its width, z −259..129 of −300..170 (v 0.088-0.912).
  - So the game's "photo position adjustment" moves the photo inside a fixed window.
- **The frame** (`profile_card00/01`) is 538 × 880 (portrait) or 880 × 538 (landscape), as the game's card JPEGs (P2).
- **The ui_text boxes:** 0 song, 1 costume, 2 badge text, 3 player name, 4 member name, 5 Friendship level, 6-8 skill lines (portrait); 9-17 the same for landscape.
- **Frame textures:** the 20 initial frames are `pfc_frame_000_<member %03d>` and `pfc_frame_h_000_<member>`.

**The Profile Card menu, `uil0090_ProcMenuLayout00`:**
- **The buttons** (`uip0090_ProcMenuBut00_00`-`_02`): each part holds all three kinds (`plate_change00`, `edit00`, `user00`) in an "on" and an "off" form.
- **Messages:** 680 "Current Profile Card", 681 "Current Accolade / Nameplate", 683 "Card-setting member", 706 "Menu".
- **Placement:** the button group's parts carry absolute positions while uil0090 also places the group at (1336, −673). Drawn as placed it is off screen, so the port moves the group back (*the game's rule is not traced*).

## Change

**New `src/app/profile_card.*`:**
- **`MakeProfileCardArt`** resolves a `save::ProfileCard` to its pictures and texts:
  - the frame's member;
  - the accolade, badge and name plate pictures; the badge text and the player name;
  - the song (live ÷ 1000) and the costume (member card ÷ 10 mod 1000);
  - the member's name (messages 197-216);
  - the three skill lines "<name> Lv. n", or "Skill not set." (message 684).
- **`ApplyProfileCard`** fills the placed part: portrait or landscape, by hiding the other layout.
- **`MakeCardPhoto`** builds the card photo texture: the photo shifted by `crop` along the free axis (x portrait, y landscape) and transparent outside the window. The port draws no stencils, so this stands in for them.

**`RunProfileMenu`** (`src/app/profile_screen.cpp`). Collection → Profile Card now opens the menu, not Change Accolade or Name Plate:
- **On the left:** the current card, drawn from its parts at 0.76 scale. The game shows its saved JPEG there.
- **On the right:** the accolade and nameplate.
- **The buttons:**
  - Change Accolade or Name Plate opens the M7 screen and comes back to the menu;
  - Create a New Profile Card, only with a photo (`profile::CanCreateCard`);
  - Everyone's Profile Cards (online) cannot be chosen.
- **"Card-setting member"** tag; the group background follows the member.
- **Log:** `profile menu: a card|no card, create open|greyed`.

**Engine:** `UiScreen::DrawPart(..., scale)`.

**Shortcuts** (in code comments):
- the Friendship heart shows 1;
- a button that cannot be chosen shows its "off" form (the game greys it);
- the dissolve backgrounds behind the game's card JPEG are not drawn;
- the card-setting member's icon keeps the part's default;
- Create only logs until P6b.
