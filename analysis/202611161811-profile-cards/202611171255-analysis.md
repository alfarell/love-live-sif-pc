# P4c: View Live Show photos

Date: 2026-11-17 12:55 (Asia/Jakarta). Part P4c of `plan/202611161811-m12-profile-cards/202611162142-plan.md`. Follows P4b (`202611171227-analysis.md`).

## Change

**Taking a photo** (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`):
- ○ (P) in View Live Show takes a photo, up to 5 per live.
- **The next frame is captured after the 3D scene and before the HUD**, so the photo is the 3D view only, as in the game (P2: its photos have no HUD).
- **Thumbnails:** each photo becomes a HUD thumbnail (a quarter size, every 4th pixel) in `uip0016_ScreenShot00`, and the counter shows "Photos n/5".
- **Log:** `live view: photo N taken (WxH, camera <mode>)`.
- `LiveResult::photos` hands the pictures (RGBA) to the caller.

**Saving** (`src/app/main.cpp`, `SavePhotos`): after a View Live Show (finished or left with Esc), each photo:
- goes to `profile::NextPhotoSlot`;
- is written to `photos/NN.png` in the data folder;
- gets a `photo` line in the save:
  - **member card:** the played member's R card in the costume she wore, `(member × 1000 + costume) × 10`;
  - **live:** song × 1000 + variant (as the game's 340011);
  - **time:** now;
  - **favourite:** off.
- P4d puts the End screen and its favourite step before this.

**Engine:**
- **`Renderer::CaptureFrame` / `TakeCapture`** (the frame read back from the GPU into memory):
  - It ends the current pass and queues `SDL_DownloadFromGPUTexture` of the swapchain image into a transfer buffer, then goes on with a 2D pass. Later draws, the HUD and the fade, land on screen but not in the copy.
  - `EndFrame` submits with a fence and waits for it, then keeps the pixels: BGRA turned into RGBA, alpha opaque. The wait costs one frame's stall, only when a photo is taken.
- **`png::Encode` / `png::Write`** (`src/engine/formats/png.*`): a small PNG writer (RGBA8, one zlib stream, CRC per chunk). zlib was already linked for the dump's archives, so there is no new dependency.
- **`UiScreen::SetMaterialTexture(instance, material, const Texture*)`:** a caller-owned texture.
- **`SceneModel::DrawUi`:** a mesh whose material names no texture (normally a text box) is drawn when the caller supplies a texture for that material. The thumbnails' materials (`screen_shot_0N_M`) name none in the game's file.

**Shortcuts** (in code comments):
- Photos are taken at the window's size, not the game's fixed 1920 × 1080.
- Photos are PNG, not JPEG.
- The thumbnail is unfiltered.
- The `photo` line's stage is the port's stage model number (stNNN), not the game's stage id.
