## Photos

- **Storage:** save slot 1 (`sce_sdmemory1`, 21 MiB): plain JPEGs, **1920 × 1080**, one every **0x57800 bytes** (350 KiB per slot). The five photos took 0x4B07D to 0x532C6 bytes each.
- **Metadata:** in the main save at **+0x60000**, one 0x100-byte record per slot, with the photo count at **+0x61E00** (5).

  | Offset | Content |
  |---|---|
  | +0x00 | 1 = used |
  | +0x04 | member card (10030 = Honoka, School Unity, R) |
  | +0x08 | 340011 (song 0340 and 011; *hypothesis:* song × 1000 + stage or chart) |
  | +0x0C | 1 = favourite |
  | +0x80 | JPEG size, a 16-byte hash (*likely* MD5), the date and time (year as u16, month, day, hour, minute, second) |

  - a 4-column grid, newest first, with a green tick on favourites and "Favorites: 1 (Maximum Number: 25)";
