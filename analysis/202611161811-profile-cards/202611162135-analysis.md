# P3: data and save for photos and profile cards

Date: 2026-11-16 21:35 (Asia/Jakarta). Step P3 of the M12 plan (`plan/202611161811-m12-profile-cards/202611161939-plan.md`). Uses P1 (`202611162102-analysis.md`) and P2 (`202611161939-analysis.md`, `202611162004-analysis.md`).

## Change

**New module `src/game/profile/`** (game rules, apart from the engine):
- **Readers:**
  - `s_profile_card_frame_datas` → `Frame`: id, initial, member, name in 4 languages;
  - `ll3::s_preset_profilecard` → `Preset`: member card, skills, frame, live, accolade, badge, plate, card picture, player name in 4 languages.
- **Rules:**
  - `OwnedFrames(member)`: the member's initial frame plus task-reward frames (type 5) of that member;
  - `CanCreateCard`: at least one photo (P2, measured);
  - `NextPhotoSlot`: the lowest free slot of 30, else the oldest non-favourite;
  - `PhotosNewestFirst` (the gallery order);
  - `PhotoFile(slot)` = `photos/NN.png`.
- **NPC pick:** `XorShift128` (the eboot's generator and start state) and `PickSupport(group)`: two different presets of the played group (`FUN_001d63d0`).
- **Shortcut** (in the docs): the pick follows the eboot's rule, but the game's generator state is shared with other code, so a run's exact picks cannot be reproduced. A simulation of the first picks after a start gave Umi + Rin; the run had Nico + Umi.

**Save (`src/game/save/`):**
- `Photo` (time, stage, member card, live, favourite) per slot: line `photo <slot> <time> <stage> <member card> <live> <favourite>`.
- One `ProfileCard` (frame, landscape, crop, live, member card, accolade, badge, plate, three skill/level pairs): line `profile_card …`.
- The field set follows the game's own debug dump ("LiveViewSS", "MyProca").
- **The pictures are files:**
  - photos: `photos/NN.png` (written in P4);
  - the card's photo: `profile_card.png` (copied when the card is saved, P6), so later photos do not change a saved card.

**Docs:** `docs/formats/card.md` now covers the preset fields and pick rule, the frame table, the game's photo and card save layout, and the port's two new save lines.
