# P4b: View Live Show cameras

Date: 2026-11-17 12:27 (Asia/Jakarta). Part P4b of `plan/202611161811-m12-profile-cards/202611162142-plan.md`. Follows P4a (`202611162152-analysis.md`). Reference: shadPS4 shots `work/m12/shad2/v05`-`v11`.

## Change

**Camera modes** (`src/app/live_screen.cpp`, `CameraRig`). R1 (Page Down) cycles:
- **MV:** the song's own camera (unchanged).
- **Member:**
  - an orbit around the followed member's head (`Bip001_Head`), at 1.1 m with a narrower lens;
  - **yaw 0 is in front of her:** her facing comes from the line between her eye controls (`Bip001_eye_L_ctrl`, `_R_ctrl`). It is eased (×3 per second) so the camera follows her turns without snapping, and it jumps at once when the member changes.
- **View:** an orbit around the stage centre (1 m up) from the front, at 9 m.

**Keys in Member and View** (held; the movement scales with frame time):
- Arrows (the left stick on a pad): orbit, yaw and pitch.
- L1 (Page Up): held alone moves the camera away, as in the game's `v08`; with Up it comes nearer.
- L2 (Home): held alone raises the look point; with Down it lowers it.
- Member only: D-pad ←/→ (comma/period on the keyboard) switches members in dance order.

**HUD:**
- the Member camera's member icons (`uip0017_CharaOrder00`, cells of `ui_medley_ch_icon00`, 5 × 4 in member order);
- the camera icon over the followed member.

**Logs:**
- `live view: member camera on member N`;
- `live view: <mode> camera yaw … pitch … distance … height …` when a movement key is released.

**Engine:** `SceneModel::NodePosition(name)`, a node's position after the last pose.

**Pad:**
- In `PadLayout::View` the left stick sends arrow keys, one per axis, past a third of the way.
- D-pad ←/→ send comma/period (Switch Members). D-pad ↑/↓ are not used.

**Route player:** `key NAME HOLD` holds a key (e.g. `key right 600ms`). The default tap stays 60 ms. New keys `,` and `.`.

**Shortcuts** (in code comments):
- The game's camera distances, limits and speeds are not traced; the port's values are chosen to look like its shots.
- The game's L1/L2 rule is only seen for L1 held alone.
