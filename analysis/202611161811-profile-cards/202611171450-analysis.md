## Change

**New `src/app/profile_edit.*`, `RunProfileEdit`.** Create a New Profile Card in the menu opens it.

- **Tab bar** (`uil0051`): Card Frame, Photo, Accolade, Name Plate, Badge, Skill Card, Finish Editing.
  - Left/Right (or L1/R1) pick a tab; Down or Enter enters its list; Up from the first row or Esc goes back to the bar.
- **The lists:**
  - **Card Frame:** the member's owned frames, each portrait and landscape.
  - **Photo:** the photos, newest first, with thumbnails.
  - **Accolade, Name Plate, Badge:** the M7 lists.
  - **Skill Card:** the member's owned skill cards. Enter sets up to three in order (①②③); Enter again removes one, and the later ones move up.
  - A star marks the chosen item. The frame, photo and skill lists use the collection cursors as the focus mark.
- **Completed Image:** the card is drawn live with the P6a helper, about 0.72-0.74 of its size.
- **Photo position adjustment** (□ on the Photo tab): the photo-adjust window (`uil0054`) with the whole photo. The arrows move the photo inside the card's window (portrait: left/right; landscape: up/down; clamped), and the card preview follows.
- **Starting values:**
  - **Editing:** the saved card.
  - **A first card:**
    - the member's first owned frame, portrait;
    - the newest photo;
    - the current accolade, badge and name plate;
    - her set skill cards.
- **Finish Editing:**
  - saves the card (`profile_card` line: frame, landscape, crop, the photo's live and member card, the plates, the skills with their levels when saved);
  - copies the chosen photo to `profile_card.png`;
  - goes back to the menu, which then shows the card.
- **Esc on the tab bar** leaves without saving.

**Engine and helpers:**
- `MakeThumb` is shared by the gallery and the editor.
- The frame slot's portrait-shaped selection glow (`uip0016_IconBack00`) is hidden; the cursor part marks the focus.
