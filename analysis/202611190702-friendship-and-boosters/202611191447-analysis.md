## Change

**`event::SetToday` and the `--date YYYY-MM-DD` option:** the in-game date for tests. Without it, the system date is used.

**After a played live (`main.cpp`):** if the played member's event is running (`event::EventYear`):
- `LivePoints(variant, rank)` are added to `save.birthdays[member]`, capped at 9999;
- a new year's event starts from 0;
- log: `birthday: member M +P points (T of 1000)`.

**New `src/app/event_screen.{h,cpp}`, `RunBirthdayEvents`** (Event → Ongoing Events, top-menu entry 21):
- **The list:**
  - the plates of the save's mode, with the group's backgrounds and the title;
  - each plate: "Event Duration" and her dates in the game's form ("Jul 25th 0:00 to Aug 1st 23:59"; the running window, else this year's), and her banner;
  - "Now Open!" while her event runs, and "Achieved!" at 1000 points;
  - arrows move the focus, Enter opens her page, and Esc leaves.
- **The page:**
  - the background layout and the title atlas row;
  - the status (770 running / 771 not held);
  - "Current Progress N points", "Achieved with 1000 points", and "You need N more birthday points.";
  - the gauge filled to N/1000;
  - her two skill cards (one for Sarah and Leah) and the medal: gold "Achieved!" at 1000, silver below;
  - "Receive rewards" while she has reached 1000, her event runs, and the reward is not received;
  - she stands in 3D on the left (`Navigator`, the costume screen's camera), instead of the part's picture.
- **Receive rewards (Enter):**
  - adds the two skill cards (`card::AddSkill`);
  - gives one of each of the row's six key items. *Shortcut:* the amount is not traced, and the game's page shows no item icons, so `uil0017` stays hidden.
  - marks the event received (776 shows).
- **Logs:**
  - `events: N events of mode M, today Y-M-D`;
  - `events: member M page`;
  - `events: member M rewards received: …`.

**The top menu:**
- while a birthday event of the member's mode runs, the "Now Open!" panel (`uip0002_MenuBG00` `event_info00_M1`) shows her `top_event_bd00_00_MM` banner;
- log: `top menu: birthday event of member M running`;
