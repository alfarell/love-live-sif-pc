**Notation:**

- `S` = `G + 0x8F720`, the main save (`FUN_00071de0`). `FUN_00074370` returns `G + 0x8F728`, so offsets from it are 8 more.
- **Member record:** `S + 0x658 + i × 0xDC`, where `i` is the member index 0-19 (N1).

## 1. Friendship (done)

**Tables** (`level_datas.bin`, loader `FUN_00078670`):
- `s_level_datas`: 200 × (level, total points);
- `s_experience_datas`: **9 rows × 7 columns** (row size 0x1C);
- `s_emblemrank_datas`: 11 × (rank, level).

The loader also asks for `s_yellpoint_datas`, which the file does not have.

**Accessors:**

| Function | Role |
|---|---|
| `FUN_00078860(…, rank, difficulty)` | `s_experience_datas[difficulty][rank]`: the base gain |
| `FUN_000787f0(level)` | the level row |
| `FUN_00078830(points)` | the level for a total |
| `FUN_00078880(level)` | the emblem rank: the highest rank whose level ≤ the given value |

**The gain after a live** (`FUN_000a68d0`, `0xA6DEA`-`0xA702D`):
1. **Who:** only the played member (the live's `+0xB6E`).
2. **Base:** `exp[difficulty index G+0xF08C8][rank index G+0xF0914]`. The difficulty indexes are 0-8, as M11: arcade variants 0-4, home variants 10-13 → 5-8. The ranks are D … SSS.
3. **Skills,** each added as `base × sum / 100` when `base × sum ≥ 100`:
   - `FUN_000cacf0`: kind 18, More Friendship Points;
   - kind 19 (More Friendship Points +);
   - kind 20 (Expanded Friendship Points).
4. **Perfect Points** (kind 21, `FUN_000cad10`): `+ round(sum / 100 × the live's Perfect count +0xC72)` (`0x5E8734` = 100).
5. **The level** comes from the new total. The result keeps:
   - the level before (`+0x8`);
   - the points gained (`+0xC`);
   - the new total (`+0x10`);
   - the new level (`+0xA`).
6. **Not in View Live Show** (`+0xC96`): no gain.

**Saved** by `FUN_0007cf30`, after the live, in the member record:
- `+0x10`: lives played with her, +1;
- `+0x1C`: Friendship points total.
- The total before the live is kept at `G + 0x8EFA8` for the result's bar.

**Emblems** (the Friendship "heart" and badge):
- `FUN_000de7f0` draws `emblem_<member>_<rank>` (`ui/ui_yellrank.pac`, 11 ranks × 20 members = 220 pictures; 444 entries with the language copies) from a profile card's `favorability` through `FUN_00078880`.
- `favorability` therefore holds the **Friendship level**, and the rank picks the picture. *Hypothesis:* "different Badges" are these emblems.
- Other users of the rank: `0x153AF1` and `0x19A08F` (not traced).

## 2. Wai-Wai! Boosters (done)

- **The count:** one save field, `S + 0x554`, from 0 to 9. The header's "Wai-Wai! Booster N" (698) and "9 MAX" (699) are this count.
- **Sources** (all capped at 9):

| Source | Code |
|---|---|
| **Login bonus** (§3): the odd days of the 12-day cycle | `FUN_001b94c0` (top menu) |
| **Drops after a live:** +1 per booster drawn (Drop Support pools, M11) | `FUN_0007cf30`, `0x7D637` |
| **Mission reward type 3:** + the row's amount | `FUN_001c7100` case 3 |

  - **The result** shows a booster reward (`reward_booster00`, case 3 of `FUN_00115fc0`) and flags "over 9" when the count would pass 9.
  - **M7 read type 3 as a badge** (every row has id 5003). In the eboot, case 3 adds boosters and does not read the id. **The port's type-3 badge is therefore wrong.** Fix in F7.
- **Use:**
  - Prepare sets the live request's flag `+0x12C` (`G + 0x4644CC`, at `0x177947`);
  - after the live, `FUN_0007cf30` clears the flag and takes one booster (`0x7DA12`);
  - the material count gets the booster's +10 (M11, `s_CommonParam +0x1F0`).
- **The "used" counter** for mission kind 30 is not traced. *Hypothesis:* it is counted at `0x7DA12`.

## 3. The login bonus (found here; message 18's mission kind)

In `FUN_001b94c0`, the top menu's refresh, once per day (`FUN_00075690(0)`):
- `S+0x558` = the login date;
- `S+0x568 + group × 4` += 1: the logins per group;
- the reward is the day `S+0x55C` (0-11) of the table at `0x5F4350` (12 × (µ's, Aqours)); then `day = (day + 1) mod 12`.
  - **Entry 0** (days 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11): **+1 booster** (cap 9).
  - **Otherwise** (days 2, 4, … 12): **+10 of that key item** (µ's 29-34, Aqours/Saint Snow 55-60; cap 9999).
- The column is the group mode: µ's, or Aqours and Saint Snow.

Mission kind 18, "Perfect Attendance Award!", counts login bonuses.

## 4. Birthday events (done)

**Tables:**
- `s_bd_event_datas` (`0xE03898`, by member index, `FUN_00010330`);
- `s_bdEventPointDatas` (`0xE038A8`, u16 by index, `FUN_00010340`);
- the birthdays: `character_params.bin` `s_birthdayParams` (23 × (month, day) by member id, e.g. Honoka 8/3, Leah 12/12; `FUN_00012940`).

**The window** (`FUN_0000f3d0`, for each member of the current group):
- **from** 7 days before her birthday (`− 0x93A80` s = 604800 s) **to** her birthday at 23:59:59, by the in-game clock;
- a January birthday before the 7th uses the next year's date.
- **Event images:** `top_event_bd00_00_<member>` (the top menu's event panel).

**Points** (`FUN_0000f850`):
- **After a played live** with her in her window: `pt[2] + pt[3 + d] + pt[12 + rank]`.
  - `d` is the variant (0-4 arcade; 10-13 home give 5-8).
  - `pt` = 10 10 0 | 5 5 7 10 12 | 5 7 10 12 | 2 3 4 5 6 7 8.
  - **So:** arcade EASY … CHALLENGE 5/5/7/10/12; home BEGINNER … ADVENTURE 5/7/10/12; rank D … SSS 2 … 8.
- **Two other cases:** case 1 gives `pt[1]` = 10, and case 4 gives `pt[0]` = 10.
  - *Hypothesis:* View Live Show and Medley.
  - *Hypothesis:* 34 "Log in during this member's BD event" is the case-4 login.
- **Stored:**
  - in her record `+0xB8` (u16, cap 9999);
  - in a running total `S + 0x61FD0 + i × 0xC`.
- **The year:** her record keeps the event's year at `+0xBC`. A new year's event resets the points and clears flag bits 1-2 of `+0xBA`. Bit 0 survives (*hypothesis:* "has had an event").

**Not traced:**
- the reward claim at 1000 (773-776): which flag bit, and that the rewards are the row's skill cards (fields 2-3) and key items (4-9);
- the Ongoing Events screen (`50_event`).

  These are left for F2.
