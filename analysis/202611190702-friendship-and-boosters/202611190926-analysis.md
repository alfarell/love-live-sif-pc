# F3: data and save for Friendship, boosters, the login bonus and birthday events

Date: 2026-11-19 09:26 (Asia/Jakarta). Step F3 of `plan/202611190702-m14-friendship-and-boosters/202611190702-plan.md`. Follows F2 (`202611190801-analysis.md`).

## Code

**New `src/game/friendship/friendship.{h,cpp}`:**
- **`Load`:** `level_datas.bin`: 200 levels (level 200 = 2,200,000 points), the 9 × 7 gains and the 11 emblem ranks.
- **Helpers:**
  - `LevelOf(points)`, `PointsFor(level)` and `EmblemRank(level)`, as the eboot's accessors;
  - `EmblemPath(member, rank)` → `ui/ui_yellrank.pac/texture.pac/emblem_<member>_<rank>`.
- **`Gain(difficulty, rank, skills, perfects)`:** the F1 §1 formula.
  - The base comes from the table.
  - Kinds 18-20 each add `base × sum / 100` when that is at least 1.
  - Kind 21 adds `ceil(sum / 100 × perfects)`: the eboot's `vroundss` mode 2 rounds up.

**New `src/game/event/event.{h,cpp}`:**
- **`Date`:** `Today()`, `AddDays` (std::chrono) and `ParseDate("YYYY-MM-DD")` for a later `--date` test option.
- **`Load`:**
  - the 20 events (`s_bd_event_datas`): member, goal, skill cards and key items;
  - the birthdays (`character_params.bin` `s_birthdayParams`);
  - the 19 points (`s_bdEventPointDatas`).
- **`Active(birthday, today)` and `Window`:**
  - the window runs from 7 days before her birthday to her birthday;
  - a birthday early in January belongs to the window that starts in December.
- **`LivePoints(variant, rank)`:** as the eboot's `FUN_0000f850`.
- **`kLoginBonus`:** the 12-day table (`0x5F4350`); `kMaxBoosters` = 9; `kBoosterUnits` = 10.

**Save (`save.{h,cpp}`):**

| Line | Field |
|---|---|
| `friendship MEMBER POINTS LIVES` | `bonds[member]`: Friendship points and lives played with her |
| `boosters HELD USED` | `boosters` (0-9) and `boosters_used` (for mission kind 30) |
| `login YYYYMMDD DAY COUNT` | the last login bonus, the next day of the cycle (0-11), and bonuses in all (kind 18) |
| `birthday MEMBER YEAR POINTS RECEIVED` | `birthdays[member]`: her latest event's year, points and reward flag |

Each line is written only when it has something to say, so older saves read with zeros.
