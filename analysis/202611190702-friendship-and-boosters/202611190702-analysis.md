## Friendship

**The tables:** `common/common.pac/game.pac/level_datas.bin`.

| Symbol | Content |
|---|---|
| `s_level_datas` | 200 rows of (level, points needed in total): level 1 = 0, 2 = 250, 3 = 505, 4 = 766 … (8 bytes each) |
| `s_experience_datas` | 63 i32: *hypothesis* 9 difficulty indexes × 7 ranks (D … SSS), e.g. 160, 210, 235, 260, 285, 310, 335 for the first row; the last is 0 |
| `s_emblemrank_datas` | 11 rows of (rank, Friendship level): 1/1, 2/20, 3/30, 4/40, 5/50, 6/100, 7/120, 8/130, 9/140, 10/150, 11/200. *Hypothesis:* the badge ranks ("different Badges") |

**What earlier traces found:**
- **M11 (`analysis/202611151530-skills-and-cards/202611161517-analysis.md`):** the Friendship gain is `FUN_00078860(save +0xf0914, difficulty)`. `+0xf0914` is the 7-valued rank index, so the gain is read by rank and difficulty, which fits `s_experience_datas`.
- **The Friendship skills** (M11 kinds 18-21: More Friendship Points, Expanded Friendship Points, Perfect Points) add to the gain. They have no effect in the port yet.
- **The profile card** (M12): its record has a `favorability` field. The card draws the Friendship heart at level 1 (*shortcut*).
- **The result's Items Obtained** shows "Friendship Points Obtained" (546) and "To Next Level" (547) with a level bar (`uip0010_PlayerLevel00`, hidden in the port, M11).
- **Mission conditions** of the Friendship kinds wait for this (M7 analysis 1839: kinds 21, 25, 27, 28, 30, 32, 42 include boosters and Friendship).
- **Badges** (M7 1140): "Badge conditions: Friendship levels. `level_datas.bin` and `s_experience_datas` are not read."

**Texts:** 546, 547; 553 "Badge"; 554 "%s → %s"; 555 "Lv.%2d"; `"Level: %s"`.

## Wai-Wai! Booster

**What it is:** an item the player holds (no data table found).
- **Prepare for the Live Show** (M11 S2 capture): "Wai-Wai! Booster: You Have 3" in the right column; □ "Use Wai-Wai! Booster".
- **Header:** the top menu reads "Wai-Wai! Booster N" (M7: N = 1; N2: N = 4). *Hypothesis:* the number held. Messages 698 "Wai-Wai! Booster %1d" and 699 "Wai-Wai! Booster 9 MAX", so at most 9.
- **The effect** (M11 `202611161517`): a used booster adds 10 material units after the live (`s_CommonParam +0x1f0` = 10; flag `+0x2D`). The Wai-Wai! Booster Boost skill (kind 22) adds 10 more with its chance.
- **How to get one:** Drop Support (kind 23) gives "a 10% chance of getting a Wai-Wai! Booster". The M11 drop function has pools 1-2 for boosters (`FUN_000a68d0`), not ported.
  - *Not known:* other sources (login, missions, a time refill?).
  - Messages 700-702 "Current Progress %4d points" sit next to 698/699: *hypothesis*, a booster gauge filled by points.
- **The save:** the M7 `own` type 3 turned out to be badges, not boosters. Where the count is kept is not traced.

**UI strings:** `boost_item00` (header), `reward_booster00` (result), `booster_icon00`.

## Birthday events

**`common/common.pac/bd_event.pac`:**

`bd_event_data.bin`, `ll3::bd_event::s_bd_event_datas`: 20 rows of 64 bytes, one per member index.

| Field | Content (row 0, Honoka) |
|---|---|
| 0 | member index 0-19 |
| 1 | 1000: the points goal (773 "Achieved with 1000 points") |
| 2, 3 | 12024, 13025: two skill cards (the rewards, "special Skill Cards"); the last two members have one |
| 4-9 | 29-34 (µ's) or 55-60 (Aqours, Saint Snow): the group's key items |
| 12-14 | 65537 each (*not known*) |

`bd_event_point_data.bin`, `s_bdEventPointDatas`: 19 u16, `10 10 0 5 5 7 10 12 5 7 10 12 2 3 4 5 6 7 8`. *Hypothesis:* points per live by difficulty and rank.

- **When it runs:** around a member's birthday by the in-game clock (768 "All dates and times are in-game time"). The birthdays are messages 52-71.
- **Texts:** 469-472, 577 "Participating in BD Event", 578 "+%d points", 767 "Event Duration", 769-778.
- **UI:** `ui/ui_bd_event.pac`; `ui2/50_event` member plates (`uil0012_eve_bd_member_plateSet00`, `uip0017`, `uip0020`); top-menu entry 21 "Ongoing Events" (M6 table).
- **The live banner:** the top menu's "Now Open!" panel shows "HAPPY BIRTHDAY! HONOKA" (the port's routes show it since M6 with a fixed picture).
