## Change

**The top menu (`top_menu.cpp`):**
- **`LoginBonus(save)`:** on the day's first visit (`save.login_date` ≠ today), the next of the 12 days (`event::kLoginBonus`), F1 §3.
  - A booster, capped at 9; or 10 of the key item for the current member's group, kept as `owned` type 2 and capped at 9999.
  - Then `login_day` moves on, `logins` counts up, and the date is kept.
  - Log: `login bonus: day N: …, M boosters held`.
  - *Shortcut:* the bonus pop-up is not shown.
- **The header, box 1:** "Wai-Wai! Booster N", or "Wai-Wai! Booster 9 MAX" at 9.
  - *Shortcut:* in English, as the header date; the menu loads no messages.
- **`main.cpp`** writes the save when the login date changed, besides the mode and the chosen member.

**Prepare (`song_select.cpp`):**
- **The panel:** "Wai-Wai! Booster" (607), "You Have" (608), and the count.
- **Square (Q):** uses one booster when one is held and none is used yet.
  - The count drops at once and the "Used" ribbon shows, as the game's `p3.png`.
  - Log: `song select: Wai-Wai! Booster used (N left)`.
- **`SongChoice::booster`** carries the use to the live. The final choice keeps it.

**After a played live (`main.cpp`):**
- **The booster is spent:** `save.boosters` −1 and `boosters_used` +1 (eboot `0x7DA12`).
  - `costume::DropInput::booster` adds **10 units** (`s_CommonParam +0x1F0`), and Wai-Wai! Booster Boost (kind 22) another 10 at its chance.
  - *Shortcut:* the Boost is rolled only with a booster used; not traced.
  - Log: `boosters: one used, N left`.
- **Drop Support (kind 23):** its % chance gives one booster, capped at 9 (M11: pool 1; eboot `0x7D637`).
  - Log: `boosters: Drop Support gave one, N held`.
  - *Shortcut:* the booster is not shown on Items Obtained.
