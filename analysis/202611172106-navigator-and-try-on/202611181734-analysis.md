Date: 2026-11-18 17:34 (Asia/Jakarta). Step N4 of `plan/202611172106-m13-navigator-and-try-on/202611172106-plan.md`. Follows N3 (`202611181710-analysis.md`).

## What the room part holds

`ui2/10_top_menu` `uip0001_RoomBG00` (its strings):
- **two room meshes:** `room_bg000` and `room_bg001`, with the materials `_charaTex_bg_room00` and `_charaTex_bg_room01` (`mainmanu_bg_pic00_00_01.png` / `_01_01.png` in the file, Honoka's);
- **a quad `model_character00`** with the material `_charaImage01`.
- **So the game draws the member off-screen** (the eboot's `CharacterOffscreenRenderer`) and shows that picture on the quad, inside the room part.

## Change

**New `src/app/navigator.{h,cpp}`, `Navigator`:**
- **Model:** the member's model in her costume, from the costume pac (`member::CostumePack`, `_v1` for the special colour), with her own `model.pac` for face and hair. The group's default costume when none is worn.
- **The idle loop as the eboot (N1 §3):**
  - stand (`001`, looping) for `IdleWait` frames;
  - then `PickIdle` for her group;
  - the idle plays once, then she stands again.
  - Random numbers come from a xorshift128 with the eboot's seed.
- **`Talk()`:** plays one of `503-506` (talk1-4) once.
- **`MenuCamera(aspect)`:** fixed, fitted to the N2 shot. The side-by-side comparison is `work/m13/n4_compare.png` (not committed).
- **Logs:**
  - `navigator: member N in <model>`;
  - `navigator: talk N`;
  - `navigator: idle N`.

**Top menu (`top_menu.cpp`, `RunTopMenu(…, const MenuMember*)`):**
- **Three steps per frame:**
  1. the layout with only the room part (`DrawLayout` with per-node alpha from the new `UiScreen::Placements`);
  2. `Renderer::Use3D()` and the navigator;
  3. the layout without the room part.
- **The room:**
  - `model_character00` is hidden; the port draws her in 3D instead of a picture on the quad;
  - `room_bg000` gets the member's room `mainmanu_bg_pic00_00_MM`;
  - `room_bg001` is hidden.
- **Voice:** the voice lines are the current member's. Her greeting and T's lines start a talk motion.
- **`main.cpp`:** passes the save's current member (`save::Current`), her `wear` costume and her special colour.

**Engine:**
- **`Renderer::Use3D()`:** back to a 3D pass after 2D draws. It keeps the picture and clears the depth. `BeginPass(three_d, clear)` now takes the clear flag.
- **`UiScreen::Placements(layout)`:** lists a layout's top-level nodes and what each one places.

## Shortcuts (in code comments)

- **Clips switch at once.** The game blends over 60 frames (`s_common_param` field 2).
- **No face animation:** no blinks and no lip sync (the facial clips and `voice_*.btlb` are not used).
- **Talk motion:** a random one of `503-506`; which motion goes with which line is not traced.
- **Camera:** fitted by eye, not traced.
- **`room_bg001`:** hidden. When the game shows the second room is not traced (*hypothesis:* time of day).
