There is also a plain `mainmanu_bg_pic00_00`. *Hypothesis:* the two are the eboot's `room_bg000`/`room_bg001` layers (perhaps day and evening); N4 checks which shows.

**Motions:** every member has `001_stand` and `010_idle` in `chr/cNN/cNN.pac/motion.pac`; Honoka has `518_dresschk`. The port finds them by member and number, so no name table is needed.
