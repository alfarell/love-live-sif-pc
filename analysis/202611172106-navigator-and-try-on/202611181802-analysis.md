## Change

**New `src/app/member_popup.{h,cpp}`, `MemberPopup`:**
- **List:**
  - the mode's members, with the current member focused;
  - arrows move the focus (*shortcut:* up and down move by 4);
  - Page Up / Page Down (L1/R1) switch to the other group's list;
  - Space / Return opens the profile; Esc closes.
- **Profile:** the member's profile texts (N3 message ids), her group's background, SD figure and mark, "Continue with this member?" and OK. Space / Return chooses her; Esc goes back to the list.
- **Logs:** `member popup: open, mode N`, `… focus member M[, profile]`, `… chose member M`, `… closed`.

**Top menu:**
- **`RunTopMenu(…, save::SaveData* save)`** replaces N4's `MenuMember`. The navigator is the save's current member.
- **One function shows a member:** it reloads the navigator, sets her room, sets the header logo and plate of her group, gathers her lines and plays the greeting.
- **End (R2):** opens the popup. A chosen member sets `save.mode` to her side and `save.navigator[mode]` to her.
- **Home (L2):** toggles `save.mode`, which shows that mode's member at once.
  - Log: `top menu: mode µ's|Aqours (L2), member M`.
  - *Shortcut:* the game's loading screen between members is not shown.

**Input:** in the menu layout the controller's L2/R2 now send Home/End, as in View Live Show.

**`main.cpp`:**
- **Save:** the save is written when the mode or the chosen member changed on the menu.
- **The played member (`skills.member`)** is now `save::Current(save)` instead of Honoka (`--member` still wins), and follows the menu's choice. It reaches:
  - lives and the live intro's member card;
  - the skill cards and their drops;
  - the costume step's cards;
  - photos and profile-card editing.
