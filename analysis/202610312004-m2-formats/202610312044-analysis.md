## 2. Other findings

- **`acb`-tagged `ACGP` entries are not CRI** (1,229, in character motion archives). They are chunked command lists (`ACGP` → `ACMD`). Only the 3 cue sheets in `sound/sound.pac` are CRI ACB.
- **Song audio for song 0010 lives in the DLC bank** `sound/dlc/MUSIC_0010.awb`. The dump's `sound/dlc` and the DLC PKGs are the same data (Part 2). The port must find music per song through `music.acb`'s stream-bank table.
- `sys` sounds include 151 **ADX** waves (EncodeType 0); `sif_hca2wav` does not decode ADX yet.
- Shader permutation names (`fur_simple-00101`) look like feature bit strings; with 9 materials, the rewrite needs about 9 shader programs with feature switches.
