## Which meshes are screens

Materials of the 13 extracted stages with screens (`tools/bmar_dump.py` on `work/extract/stages_m8/`). The screens show a **placeholder texture**, and a separate dot-mask mesh (`mon_dotmask_mt`, `monitor_dotmask.tga`) lies over the main screen:

| Placeholder texture | Stages, materials |
|---|---|
| `monitor_scrnrogo.tga` (the "ラブライブ!" logo still) | st001, st004, st005, st007 `monitor_001`; st015 `mon_001`; st017 `mon_003`; st018 `monitor_001`-`012` |
| `monitor.tga` | st006 `monitor_001_mt` (its one long screen: the 1920 × 120 `_b` strip); st017 `mon_001` |
| `s01_monitor_off.tga` | st101 `monitor_001`, `monitor_001_floor`, `monitor_004` |
| `cyber_monitor_dummy.tga` | st013 `mon_001_mt`, `mon_002_mt` |

Not replaced:
- **st017 `mon_002`** (`score.tga`, a score board).
- **st102-104 `monitor`** (`tx_ef_monitor_0015.tga`): these stages animate their own screen textures with `102_ef_monitor_*` motions.

*Hypothesis:* all placeholders show the same movie. The `ll3monitor` shader was not disassembled, so how the game combines the video, the dot mask and the logo is not known. The port draws the movie in place of the placeholder and leaves the dot-mask mesh as it is.
