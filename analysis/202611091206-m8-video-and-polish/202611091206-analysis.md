## 1. The videos in the dump

All of them are loose files under `CUSA24620/movie/` (810 MB). None sits inside a `.pac`. A USM is CRI's Sofdec2 movie container: chunks tagged `@SFV` (video), `@SFA` (audio) and so on.

| Files | Count | Size, rate, length | The game's path pattern (eboot strings) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `monitor_NNN.usm`, `monitor_NNN_b.usm` (NNN 001-009, 051-053) | 22 | 512 × 288 at 30 fps, 150 frames (5 s), up to 600 frames; also 1920 × 120 and 1024 × 288 | `movie/monitor_%03d.usm`, `movie/monitor_%03d_%c.usm`, `monitor_rtt` | Live-stage screens. `monitor_rtt` suggests a render-to-texture. *Hypothesis:* `_b` and the 1920 × 120 files are LED strips |
| `music/preview_music_NNNN.usm` | 117 | 256 × 144 at 59.94 fps, about 27 s (1,623 frames for 0010) | `movie/music/preview_music_%04d.usm` | Song previews (song select) |
| `dress/preview_costume_NNN.usm` | 63 | 256 × 144 at 60 fps, 12 s (720 frames) | `movie/dress/preview_costume_%03d.usm` | Costume previews (costume screens) |

**Format** (`tools/usm_probe.py`, all 382 file entries):
- one video stream, `mpeg_codec` 1;
- **no audio, no alpha, no encryption**;
- the elementary stream has sequence headers (`00 00 01 B3`) and GOPs, but **no `00 00 01 B5` extension anywhere** in the four files checked, so it is **pure MPEG-1 video**, not MPEG-2.

The eboot also names a `MovieModule` and a `movie_update_thread`: the game decodes on its own thread.

## 3. Decoder options

| Option | Size | Licence | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **pl_mpeg** (phoboslab, one header, MPEG-1 video and MP2 audio) | About 4,000 lines | MIT | **Recommended.** It decodes exactly MPEG-1 video; it is the same kind of vendored code as clHCA. It needs a download from GitHub, which needs the user's permission |
| FFmpeg (libavcodec) | Large DLLs or a heavy build | LGPL | Too heavy for MPEG-1 only; a system-wide toolchain question |
| Own MPEG-1 decoder | About 1,500-2,000 lines | Ours | Possible, but it repeats pl_mpeg with more risk |

**Cost:** MPEG-1 at 512 × 288 or 256 × 144 is cheap; pl_mpeg decodes far larger sizes in real time. YUV → RGB can run on the CPU at these sizes. A shader is an option if it shows up in the frame cost.

## 4. Sync

None of the videos carries audio, so "in sync" means two things:
- **Stage screens:** the frame comes from the song clock, `floor(now × fps) mod frames`. This keeps the screens locked to the music and stops drift.
- **Previews:** they restart when the selection changes and then follow the wall clock. If the port later plays preview audio, use the audio clock instead.
