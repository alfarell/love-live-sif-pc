## 1. Stage-screen video: per song

The number follows the song: the same stage gave 006 for song 0340 and 005 for song 0120. The field is **`s_music_datas` +0x124** (`common/common.pac/live_common.pac/live_datas.bin`, 832-byte records); it is 6 for 0340 and 5 for 0120, and no other field matches both:

| +0x124 | Songs | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | 40, 150, 240, 280 |
| 2 | 1 | 130 |
| 3 | 3 | 20, 80, 160 |
| 4 | 22 | 50, 180, 260 … |
| 5 | 7 | 10, 60, 70, 120 … |
| 6 | 14 | 110, 220, 340 … |
| 7 | 6 | 30, 90, 100 … |
| 9 | 53 | **all Aqours and Saint Snow songs** (1010 …) |
| 51 / 52 / 53 | 5 each | 190-210, 290-310, 410-430, 620-700 |

`monitor_008` is never used. *Hypothesis:* the run-time field `+0x16C` read at eboot `0xBD4C0` is a copy of this one. That is consistent with every observation, but the copy itself was not traced.

**`_b` (stage 6):** `'a' + s_stage_datas` byte +0x2F, which is 1 only for stage 6, so stage 6 plays `monitor_NNN_b.usm`. `_b` files exist only for 001-007 and 051, so a stage-6 song with another number (9, 52, 53) would ask for a file that does not exist. *Hypothesis:* the game then shows nothing or falls back; not run.

**Picture check** (`work/m8/v0_monitor_005_compare.png`): the screen behind the members in live 0120 shows pink and yellow stripes with stars. The port's decode of `monitor_005` (`--movie`) shows the same stripes, stars and colours, which confirms both the mapping and V1's decoder colours.

## 2. Song and costume previews: the add-on store

No preview movie was opened on any screen visited. The eboot shows where they go:

- **The path builders:** `0x1CBC10` formats `movie/dress/preview_costume_%03d.usm` and `0x1CBCD0` formats `movie/music/preview_music_%04d.usm`. Both fall back to `…_001` / `…_101`, or `…_0010` / `…_1010`, when `0x1EAD0(id)` is nonzero.
- **Their one caller (`0x136531`, `0x136572`)** fills these materials:
  - `move_image00_M`: the movie picture;
  - `cd_jacket00` and `cd_jacket_bk00`;
  - `dress_card00_M%02d` and `dress_info00`;
  - `member_info00_M`;
  - `button_text00`.
- **The UI part** with a movie in its name is `ui2/02_purchase` `uip0008_TrialMove00` ("trial movie"), next to `uip0007_MemberInfo00`, `uil0007_PacPreview00`, `uip0040_Pop_DressCard00` and the store layouts.

So the 256 × 144 song and costume previews are the **store's trial movies** for add-on songs and costumes. They are not part of the song select or the costume screens.

## 3. Consequences for the M8 plan

| Step | Change |
|---|---|
| **V3, stage screens** | Fully specified: song `+0x124` gives `movie/monitor_%03d.usm`, with `_b` on stage 6. The video goes on the stage's `ll3monitor` materials; the dot mask and logo use is still to read from the shader. One video per live, so one decoder |
| **V4, previews** | **Not applicable as planned.** The port has no store. A store front for buying add-ons is out of scope: no online service. Options: drop V4, or show the trial movie in an offline "add-on info" view of costumes and songs the user does not have. That is a user decision |
| **M8 exit wording** | "song previews, monitors" becomes: stage-screen videos in lives; previews only if a screen for them is built |
