## What the port does now

**Every start with `--title`** (the normal start):
1. the logos and the caution notice;
2. **the autosave notice (691)**, until a key is pressed;
3. the title;
4. **on a new save only:** "Save data creation" (688, OK);
5. **the online notice (692)**, until a key is pressed.

**A new save** (no `save.txt` in the data folder), after the title:
- **The name entry** (`app/first_start.cpp`). It uses the PC keyboard:
  - typing, up to 10 characters, and Backspace;
  - Enter opens "Confirm Name" (696) with Back (focused) / OK;
  - an empty name gives "New Player";
  - Back returns to typing.
- **"Choose a Member"** (`MemberPopup::OpenFirst`):
  - it starts in µ's mode on Honoka;
  - R1/L1 (Page Down / Page Up) change the group;
  - ✕ opens her profile page, and OK chooses her;
  - Esc does nothing here, because a member must be chosen;
  - the 3D member on the left follows the cursor (`Navigator`, `CostumeCamera`).
- **The save** keeps the name, the member, and the mode of her group. It is written at once.

**Player's Name in Profile** (Collection → Profile Card → Change Accolade or Name Plate):
- R1 past the Badge tab, or L1 before the Accolade tab, opens the same name entry. Esc there leaves the name unchanged.
- The new name shows on the nameplate and is saved.
