## What the port does now

**The login pop-up** (`app/top_menu.cpp`, `LoginPopup`):
- **When:** on the day's first top menu (the M14 rule), the pop-up shows over the menu's left half.
- **What it shows:** the reward's icon, then its name and amount:
  - a Wai-Wai! Booster: `reward_5003`, message 552, x1;
  - or a key material: its material icon and name, x10.
- **Keys:** Enter, Space or Esc close it. The menu takes no keys while it is open.
- **For routes:** the menu reports the screen `login_bonus` until the pop-up closes. So existing routes close it with their "press enter until menu" step, unchanged.

**"Change Costume?"** (`app/costume_screen.cpp`):
- **When:** Enter (Change Clothes) on an owned costume opens "Check Costume": the costume's picture, 535 "Change Costume?", **Back** (focused) / **OK**.
- **Keys:** Left/Right move between the buttons.
- **OK** puts the costume on, with its colour and the twirl (519).
- **Back**, Esc and Backspace close the box without a change.

## Routes

- **`login_1`, `login_2`, `login_3`** (`--date`, one data folder):
  - day 1 shows the booster;
  - day 2 shows the key material x10;
  - the same day again shows no pop-up.
- **`try_on`:** Esc backs out of the question, then OK puts the costume on.
- **`costumes`:** its second Enter on an owned costume is now Back.

## Shortcuts and differences from shadPS4

- **Login text alignment:** the game centres each line ("Embroidery" over "x10"). The port's lines start at one left edge, because a text style's alignment places the whole text block, not each line.
- **No blur:** neither box blurs what's behind it.
- **The picture in Check Costume:** it shows the costume's thumbnail. The game shows the member's card, but the costume screen has no card data (a shortcut since M13).
- **The navigator's loading screen** before the pop-up is not shown (T1 hypothesis, `FUN_00263570`).
- **The first-menu tutorial picture** is left for PLAY.md (see above).
