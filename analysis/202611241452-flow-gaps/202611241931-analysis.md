Date: 2026-11-24 19:31 (Asia/Jakarta). Step T5 of `plan/202611241452-m17-flow-gaps/202611241452-plan.md`. Follows T4 (`202611241814-analysis.md`).

## What the port does now

**The song list follows the mode** (`app/song_select.cpp`):
- the mode is the one L2 sets on the top menu (`save.mode`);
- µ's mode lists group 0: 72 songs;
- Aqours mode lists groups 1 and 2 (Aqours and Saint Snow): 53 songs;
- add-ons the user doesn't own are still left out first.

**Message 591**, in Play Live Show (both modes) when the played member is not one of the song's singers (`SongInfo::members`):
1. **Confirm** shows "You cannot play (song) with this member. Would you like to change members temporarily?" with **Cancel** focused / **OK**. The English text has only the song; the Japanese text has the member, then the song.
2. **OK** opens the member box:
   - it shows the current mode's list with no group switch (the L1/R1 arrows and labels are hidden);
   - only the song's singers can be chosen, and the cursor starts on the first of them in the song's order;
   - arrows skip the others.
3. **Her profile, then OK** makes her the temporary member, and the song goes straight to Select Difficulty.
- **The exception:** song 1380 does not ask in Aqours mode (eboot `FUN_00173b60`, T1).

**The temporary member:**
- She plays the live: her skill cards, Friendship, intro and records.
- Prepare shows her skill cards.
- She is kept on the song select, its lives and Play Live Show Again.
- She ends when the top menu comes back, which resets the played member to the navigator (`main.cpp`).

## Shortcuts and hypotheses

- **The faded icons:** the member box draws non-singers faded (alpha 0.35). The game greys them (u08). The UI engine has no colour tint.
- **No loading screen:** the member loading screen (`com_load_mem00_%02d`, `FUN_00263570`) is not shown after the choice. T2 caught none either.
- **The brackets:** the English 591 text uses full-width brackets（）. The port's font draws the left one oddly; the game shows normal ones.
- **View Live Show** does not check singers: every singer dances in a viewed live. *Hypothesis:* the game doesn't check either; not seen.
- **µ's mode and song 1380:** a µ's member on 1380 can't happen in the port, because the µ's list has no Aqours songs. So the eboot's exception matters only for which Aqours member may play it.
