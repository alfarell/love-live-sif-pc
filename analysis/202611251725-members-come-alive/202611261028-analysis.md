# U4: blend shapes (format and renderer)

Date: 2026-11-26 10:28 (Asia/Jakarta). Step U4 of `plan/202611251725-m18-members-come-alive/202611251725-plan.md`. Follows U3 (`202611252253-analysis.md`).

**Term:** a *blend shape* is a set of per-vertex offsets that moves the face toward one expression (mouth "a", eyes closed). The renderer adds each shape times its weight (0-1) to the base mesh.

## The format (now in `docs/formats/bmar.md`)

The survey's guess was wrong: the shapes are not in the `BMD` part. They are textures.

**Where they live:** each shape is a btx in the model's `BMT` (texture) part.
- Format 0x1B: RGBA16F (half floats), 128 × 64.
- **Top half:** the position offset of every vertex. **Bottom half:** the normal offset.
- On Honoka, `a` moves about 700 of the 2401 face vertices, by up to 1.2 cm.

**How a face mesh finds them:**
- **Its vertex format** has bit `0x10000000`: per vertex, f32×4 = the texel-centre UVs of its position texel and normal texel. Vertex k → (k mod 128, k div 128), plus 32 rows for the normal.
- **`MESH +0x20`** is a table of (name*, 0) records, with the count at `MESH +0x60`. Each name is the shape's texture.
  - The order is shared by all members: 0 `default`, 1-7 `a i i_small u e o n`, 8-9 mouth smile/happy, 10 `eye_Blink`, 11 `eye_open`, 12-27 winks and partial blinks, 28-44 brows, 45 `mouth_Smile`.
  - Honoka has 46 shapes and Chika 51; Chika's extra 5 come after these.
- **Costume models** (`dNNN`): the face mesh keeps the texel attribute but has no table and no shape textures. They come from the member's own `mod_NN_<name>` (same vertex count).

**`ANMS`** (in the facial clips only; body motions have none):
- **Records:** as in `ANMT`, except that the record's last u32 is a u16 channel count and a u16 **shape index**. The record name is the mesh.
- **Channel:** type 12, f32 weight.
- **Examples:**
  - `facial_001_a`: shape 1, 0 → 1 over 6 frames;
  - `facial_100_blink`: shape 10, 0 → 1 at frame 4 → 0 at frame 10.

## The port

**Parser:**
- `bmar::Mesh::shapes` and `VertexLayout::shape_uv` hold the shape list and the texel attribute.
- `bmar::Motion::shapes` holds the weight tracks (name = mesh, type = shape index).

**Renderer** (`SceneModel`):
- **Loading:** a face mesh reads each shape's offsets once. They come from its own model, or from the new `shape_model` argument (the member's own model, passed by the navigator for costume models).
- **`Pose`:** collects the weights from the motion layers (the last layer wins per shape), adds them to the bind mesh and uploads it. It does this only when the weights change.

**Navigator:**
- `Face(n)` holds facial clip n (`member::FacialPath`, `mot_NN_<name>_facial_NNN_*`) at its last frame, as a layer over the body motion.
- **`--face N`** (a test option) gives every navigator that face.

## Notes for U5 / U6

- **When idle**, the game must hold `default` (shape 0): the bare mesh's open mouth is not seen in U2. U5 keeps `default` under the blinks.
- **Lives:** no face clip is played, so the faces stay as before (the bare mesh). Live faces are out of scope (plan).
- **The normal offsets** are added and then normalized (*hypothesis*: the lighting looked right in the shots).
