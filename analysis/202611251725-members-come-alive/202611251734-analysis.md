## 1. Member loading screen

**`FUN_00263570(loader, n)`:**
- Calls `FUN_00261b40(loader, 1)`: the shared loading object in mode 1. The layout comes from the table `0x5fb7a0[mode]`.
- Sets the picture node `_charaTex_splash00` to the texture `com_load_mem00_%02d` with `n`.

**Where `n` comes from:** `FUN_0025ecb0`, the member number of the member box's choice.
- µ's: 1-9.
- Aqours: 11-19.
- Saint Snow: 20 and up, plus 1, so 21-22.

These match the 20 pictures in `ui/ui_transition_character.pac`.

**Calls:** 10 call sites (`0xcff60`, `0xed623`, `0xf3c58`, `0x101e6c`, `0x10463b`, `0x108337`, `0x13d384`, `0x173e5b` (591), `0x17461c`, `0x17e1ac`). Each follows a member-box choice: R2 on the menus, 591, the first start, and the login path (`FUN_001b94c0`).

**Layout:** `00_common` `uip0072_ChrChangeSplash00`, with clips `a001` and `a002`.
- *Hypothesis:* a001 is the way in and a002 the way out.
- The background follows the group: `com_load_mem_bg_mu00_00`, `_aq00_00`, `_ss00_00`.

**Timing:** `FUN_00261b40` sets `+0x270 = 30.0`.
- *Hypothesis:* a 30-frame minimum or fade.
- The loader stays up until the new member is loaded. U2 measures how long that is.

## 5. Face: blinks and lip sync

**Facial clips are registered from a table:**
- `common/common.pac/character_common.pac/facial_datas.bin` `s_facial_anim_datas` has 54 rows of 16 bytes: id, layer, mode, flags. The id is 1000 + the clip number: 1000 default, 1001-1007 mouths, 1010/1011 smile/happy, 1100 blink, 1101-1125 eyes, 1200-1252 brows, 1300-1305, 1400.
- `FUN_0002b500` registers each one (`FUN_0002b560(controller, id, layer, flag bit 5)`).
- `facial_preset_datas.bin` `s_facial_preset_datas` (72-byte rows): expression presets. Each is an id, a count, then pairs of (clip number, weight). For example, preset 96 = brow 211 + eyes 115 + smile 10.

**Blinks (`FUN_0002b760`, the update at `0x2bb3e`):**
- A frame counter (`+0x1e40`) runs.
- When it reaches the target (`+0x1e44`), clip 1100 (blink) plays.
- Then a new target is drawn with a xorshift random between the minimum (`+0x1e4c`) and the maximum (`+0x1e48`).
- **The start values** (`0x5e4250`): target 300, maximum 300, minimum 60.
- **So:** a blink every 1 to 5 seconds at 60 fps.
- A setter (`0x2c4a0`, values × 60) can change the range; no direct caller was found.

**Lip sync: the voice timelines are mouth keys.**
- `timeline.pac/voice_01_honoka_NNNNN.btlb` (and the same data in `voice.pac/voice_*.acb`, `ACGP`/`ACMD`) hold commands of tag `0x0b`, size `0x0c`: (shape, 4, weight 1.0, frame).
  - The shape is 1-7 = `a i ismall u e o n`. These are the mouth clips 001-007.
  - *Hypothesis:* 4 = the blend frames.
- **Examples:**
  - `voice_01_honoka_10001`: 2 keys (o at 24, e at 30);
  - `10803`: 48 keys over about 440 frames.
- **So U6 plays these keys with each voice line.** The loudness *shortcut* is not needed.
- The ids follow the talk voice ids (M7).

**Still open for U4:** the shape vertices. The model names the shapes (`Honoka_face_a` …), but the BML part has no morph chunk. *Hypothesis:* they are in the `BMD` part.
