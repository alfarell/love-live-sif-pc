## A correction to M7: which voice a talk plays

M7 (`analysis/202611021940-m7-systems-breadth/202611071116-analysis.md`) read tag `0x0514` of a talk timeline as the voice and named the cue `vo_ac_<member>_<value>`. That was wrong:
- Every member's 92 talks use only 7 values in that tag, the same for all members: 501-507. These are the navigator's motion numbers (501 idle1, 502 idle2, 503-506 talk1-4, 507 login).
- So the port played one of 7 lines (`vo_ac_001_501`-`507`) for every talk since M7.

**The voice is tag `0x0028`** (payload 4). Its value plus the member's base is a `CueId` in `voice.acb`:

| Group | Base |
|---|---|
| µ's (1-9) | 10000 + 1000 × (member − 1) |
| Aqours (11-19) | 40000 + 1000 × (member − 11) |
| Saint Snow (21-22) | 50000 + 1000 × (member − 21) |

- Found from the cue table: `vo_ac_001_001` = 10001, `vo_ps_001_001` = 10701, `vo_ac_011_*` from 40003, `vo_ac_21_*` from 50003.
- 1780 of the 1840 talks have the event; 1744 resolve to a cue. The 36 that don't are µ's talks 99-102 (values 781-784).
- Example: Honoka's morning greeting, talk 50, plays `vo_ps_001_007` (10707) with motion 503.
- The voice timelines are named with the same number (`voice_01_honoka_10707.btlb`), which confirms the mapping.

## The mouth keys

`chr/cNN/cNN.pac/timeline.pac` `voice_NN_<name>_<CueId>.btlb` (`docs/formats/talk.md`). Each key is a frame (60 per second from the voice's start), then:
- tag `0x0b`: mouth shape 1-7 (`a i i_small u e o n`), blend 4 frames, weight 1.0;
- tag `0x0c`: close the mouth over 4 frames.

85 of Honoka's 89 talk voices have a timeline.

## The port

- **`game/talk`:**
  - `VoiceId` (tag 0x0028 + base) replaces `VoiceCue`;
  - `Motion` (tag 0x0514);
  - `MouthKeys` reads the voice timeline;
  - `MouthAt` cross-fades from the weights before each key to its shape over its blend frames.
- **`Say`** (top menu) plays the cue by id, then `Navigator::Talk(motion, keys)`.
  - The talk's own motion replaces the random talk1-4 (an M13 shortcut).
  - The log reads `talk 51: vo_ps_001_008 (10708)` and `navigator: talk 504, 25 mouth keys`.
- **`SceneModel::Pose`** takes extra (shape, weight) pairs after the layers. The navigator passes the mouth weights. `default` (shape 0) gets 1 − the open weight (*shortcut*: the game's facial layers and modes in `facial_datas.bin` are not traced).
