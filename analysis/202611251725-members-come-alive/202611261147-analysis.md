## What the port does now

`Navigator` (`app/navigator.cpp`) builds the face from three layers over the body motion. Each layer is a facial clip (U4); the last layer wins per shape.

| Layer | Clip | Held at | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | 0 `default` | its last frame | The bare mesh's mouth is open; U2 never shows that. `default` gives the closed neutral mouth |
| Test | `--face N` | its last frame | Test option only (U4) |
| Blink | 100 `blink` | plays once (10 frames) | Eyes shut at frame 4, open by frame 10 |

**The blink timer** follows the eboot (`FUN_0002b760`, U1 §5):
- the first blink comes 300 frames (5 s) after the member loads;
- after each blink, the next comes in 60-300 frames (1-5 s), drawn with the navigator's own xorshift instance (`random_.Below`);
- the log says `navigator: blink` each time.

The costume screen ("Trying It On") uses the same `Navigator`, so she blinks there too.

## Shortcuts

- **How the eboot draws in its range** (min + random × span, or another formula) is not traced. The port uses min..max inclusive. The range (1-5 s) matches U2's ~1.6 s gaps.
- **Blinks during a talk motion:** the port keeps blinking. U2's talk line had smiling closed eyes; that face comes with the voice keys in U6.
