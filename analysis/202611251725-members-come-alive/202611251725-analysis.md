**Terms:**
- **Blend shape** (morph target): a second copy of a mesh's vertex positions, for example the face with the mouth open on "a". The renderer mixes the base mesh with one or more shapes by weights from 0 to 1.
- **Lip sync:** moving the mouth shapes in time with a voice line.

## What the dump has (read for this survey)

**1. Face (Honoka's files; the other members follow the same naming):**
- `chr/c01/c01.pac/model.pac/mod_01_honoka.mdl` names the shapes of the mesh `Honoka_face`:
  - `Honoka_face_default`, `_a`, `_i`, `_i_small`, `_u`, `_e`, `_o`, `_n`, `_mouth_happy`, and more.
  - The BML part has no morph chunk: its chunks are NODE, DATA, MATE, MESH, MTXP, DRAW, TXTR, POF0 only. *Hypothesis:* the shape vertices are in the `BMD` part ("mad 200", undecoded since M2).
- `motion.pac` has 45 facial clips, `mot_01_honoka_facial_NNN_*.mot`:
  - mouths 001-007 (`a i ismall u e o n`), 010 `smile`, 011 `happy`;
  - 100 `blink`, 101 `eyeopen`, winks 102-105, partial blinks 110-125;
  - brows 200-252.

  Each clip is about 440 bytes and holds one `ANMS` track on `Honoka_face`. So **ANMS = blend-shape weight tracks**, which confirms the M2 hypothesis.
- `data.pac/facial_data.bin` (1.7 KB, `BLBN` with SYM entries): *hypothesis:* the expression table that maps names to shapes.
- `timeline.pac` has 252 timelines; the voice ones, such as `voice_01_honoka_10001.btlb`, are about 275 bytes each. *Hypothesis:* the mouth keys for each voice line.
