# M13: the 3D navigator, member choice and "Trying It On"

Date: 2026-11-17 21:06 (Asia/Jakarta). Survey: `analysis/202611172106-navigator-and-try-on/202611172106-analysis.md`.

**Goal:** the top menu shows the chosen member in 3D in the room. She moves, greets and talks with her voice lines. R2 switches members and L2 toggles µ's/Aqours, and the chosen member plays the lives. In Create/Change Costumes she tries on the focused costume in 3D, and L1 changes a costume to its special colour, also worn in lives.

**Exit test:**
- **Top menu:** the navigator in her own costume, with the idle motions, blinks and a talk motion with each voice line. A capture matches the game's top menu (shadPS4, the user's build).
- **Member choice:** R2 and L2 change the navigator; the choice is kept after a restart. Lives, skill cards and photos follow her, instead of always Honoka.
- **Trying It On:** the costume screen shows the member in 3D in the focused costume; "Change Clothes" keeps it.
- **Special colours:** L1 switches a costume that has one; the member wears the `_v1` colours on the top menu, in the costume screen and in lives.
- **Member names:** the member popup and its profile texts from the game's tables.
- **Checks:** all routes and unit tests pass, and the asset scan is clean.
