# M18: members come alive

Date: 2026-11-25 17:25 (Asia/Jakarta). Survey: `analysis/202611251725-members-come-alive/202611251725-analysis.md`.

**Goal:** the members on the menus look and change as in the game:
- the navigator blinks, and her mouth moves with her voice lines;
- the member loading screen after a member change (R2 and 591);
- after the first start, the nameplate shows the chosen group's badge and accolade;
- "Check Costume" shows the member's card;
- the first top menu shows the tutorial picture.

**Exit test:**
- A capture of each screen matches shadPS4, or the T2 captures.
- The face: a frame shows closed eyes in a blink, and the mouth open during a voice line.
- All routes and unit tests pass, and the asset scan is clean.

## Steps

### U1: rules (eboot and data, no port code)
- When the loading screen shows, for how long, and its animation (`FUN_00263570`, `uip0072_ChrChangeSplash00`).
- The starting badge and accolade per group.
- The costume → member card link.
- When the tutorial shows (first menu only? each group?), and which of `tutorial_000/001_00/01` it uses.
- Blink timing: the eboot's random interval, and which of clips 100-125 it uses.
- **Output:** an analysis file; anything not found is a stated *hypothesis*.

### U2: shadPS4 run (needs the user's approval; the user's build)
- Close-up captures of the navigator's face: a blink, and a voice line (frame by frame).
- An R2 member change (the loading screen), the first start with an Aqours member (nameplate, tutorial), and "Check Costume".
- The emulator's save folder is backed up first and restored after, as in M17 T2.

### U3: the small screens
- The loading screen after R2 and after 591's choice.
- The nameplate starters per group.
- The tutorial picture on the first top menu.
- The member card in "Check Costume".
- **Routes:** a member change; `first_start_1` with an Aqours member; `try_on`.

### U4: blend shapes (format and renderer)
- Decode the `BMD` shape data, and `ANMS` in `bmar.cpp`; document both in `docs/formats/bmar.md`.
- Mix the shapes in the skinned vertex path.
- **Check:** `--show` of the face with shape `a` at weight 1, against a shadPS4 close-up; a unit test of the decoder.

### U5: blinks
- Random blinks from the facial clips, at the eboot's interval, on the top menu and in "Trying It On".
- **Check:** a route capture of a closed-eye frame.

### U6: lip sync
- Decode the `voice_*.btlb` timelines. If they hold mouth keys, play them with each voice line.
- Otherwise, a *shortcut*: the voice's loudness opens the mouth (`a`).
- **Check:** captures during a talk line, compared with U2.

### U7: close
- PLAY.md, a new CHECKLIST.md section, all routes, unit tests, the asset scan, and the roadmap.

**Order:** U1 → U2 → U3 → U4 → U5 → U6 → U7. U1 and U2 can swap. U3 does not need U4.

## Out of scope
- Faces in lives (dance): the same shapes would work there, but live facial timing comes from the song timelines. This is left for later.
- Motion blending; the Aqours skin shading.

## Risks
- **The BMD layout may be hard to decode.** *Mitigation:* the shape names give anchors, and the vertex count of `Honoka_face` is known from MESH. If U4 stalls, the user decides whether to keep the small screens (U3) and close M18.
- **Voice timelines may not hold mouth keys.** *Mitigation:* the loudness shortcut in U6.
