# M12: View Live Show, photos and profile cards

Date: 2026-11-16 18:11 (Asia/Jakarta). Survey: `analysis/202611161811-profile-cards/202611161811-analysis.md`.

**Goal:** the player watches a live in View Live Show and takes photos with the Camera. The photos appear in Collection → Photo Gallery. With a photo, the player creates profile cards (frame, photo, accolade, name plate, badge, member card, skill cards, name). The card in use shows in Prepare for the Live Show beside the NPC support cards. Collection → Member Card lists the owned member cards.

**Exit test:**
- **View Live Show:** a song plays with its stage, members and movie, without notes or judging. The Camera takes a photo, which is saved and listed in the Photo Gallery after a restart.
- **Profile cards:** "Create a New Profile Card" opens once the S1/S2 rule holds. A card is created, edited, kept after a restart and set as "Profile Card in Use". It draws like the game's card (compared with a shadPS4 capture and with the preset JPEGs).
- **Lives:** Prepare shows the card in use and the two NPC support cards. Support cards with skills act at their share (M11's `+0x08`), if the presets or S1 give them skills.
- **Collection → Member Card:** owned member cards, matching a shadPS4 capture.
- **Checks:** all routes and unit tests pass, and the asset scan is clean.

## Steps

### P1: eboot trace (no code)
- **View Live Show:** its entry (top-menu id 2), the flow, the Camera controls and the photo capture (size, crop, HUD or not, JPEG writer). Where the 30 photos are stored and named.
- **Profile cards:** the record in the save, the 20 slots, the card in use, the create rule, the JPEG render.
- **Support cards:** how a live picks the NPC presets offline; preset fields 2-7 and 9.
- **Frames:** how non-initial frames are earned.
- **Output:** an analysis file. Anything not found is listed as blocked, with what is needed.

### P2: shadPS4 run (needs the user's approval)
- Play View Live Show, use the Camera, save photos. Capture the Camera screen and the "Photos taken this time" page.
- Capture Collection → Photo Gallery, Member Card and Profile Card, then create a profile card through every tab (frame, photo with framing, accolade, name plate, badge, skill card, name).
- Capture Prepare with the card in use and the support cards.

### P3: data and save
- **Readers:** frames and presets (`src/game/card/` or a new `src/game/profile/`).
- **Save:** photos (files beside the save, up to 30), profile cards (up to 20), the card in use.
- **Tests:** readers and the save round trip.

### P4: View Live Show and the Camera
- **The live without play:** the live screen with no notes, no judge and no result, from the "View Live Show" entry and its song select.
- **The Camera:** the P1/P2 controls; a photo is the rendered frame read back from the GPU and saved as a file.
- **Engine:** a frame readback in the renderer (Vulkan copy to a host buffer) and a JPEG or PNG writer (stb, already used for images if present; else a small vendored writer).
- **Route:** View Live Show with a photo taken.

### P5: Collection → Photo Gallery and Member Card
- The Photo Gallery list with preview, favourite and delete.
- The Member Card list (owned member cards: the costumes the player owns).
- Routes for both.

### P6: profile cards
- **The card drawing:** `uip0010_ProfileCard00` with the frame, photo, texts and skills; Friendship shows level 1 (*shortcut* until Friendship exists).
- **Profile Card menu** (`uil0090`, skipped in M7) and **Create / Edit** (`uil0050`): the tabs Card Frame, Photo (with framing), Accolade, Name Plate / Badge, Skill Card, Player's Name. The M7 Change Accolade screen joins this.
- **Routes:** create, edit and keep a card.

### P7: lives and close
- **Prepare:** Profile Card Selection (the 20 cards), Profile Card in Use, Profile Card Support with the NPC presets.
- **Support skills:** at their share and half duration (M11 S2), if any preset has skills.
- **Close:** PLAY.md, a new CHECKLIST.md section, all routes and unit tests, the asset scan, the roadmap.

**Order:** P1 → P2 → P3 → P4 → P5 → P6 → P7. P1 and P2 can swap if the eboot trace stalls.

## Out of scope (later)
- Live Show Medley (set lists).
- Friendship points and levels, boosters, events.
- Everyone's Profile Cards and uploads (online).
- Non-initial frames, unless P1 finds an offline source.

## Risks
- **Frame readback** is new engine work (Vulkan). *Fallback:* save the photo from the next presented frame.
- **The Camera** may be a free 3D camera with its own controls. *Fallback:* the live's own camera timeline, with the photo taken from it.
- **The create rule** may need a photo. Routes then need a seed photo file, made by the port itself in a route.
