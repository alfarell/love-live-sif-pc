## P4a: the flow
- **Top menu:** Play → View Live Show (entry 2) opens the song select in a view mode.
- **Song select steps:** song (with the banner and the caution note), stage, costume, then Prepare with the help panel and LIVE START. There is no difficulty step.
- **The live:** stage, members, stage screens and the song's camera; no notes, judge or score. The HUD is `uil0002_LiveView00`: camera mode, photo count, song name.
- **After the song:** back to the View Live Show song select. The End screen comes in P4d.
- **Route:** `live_view` (top menu → View Live Show → a song → the live runs → it ends).

## P4b: camera modes
- **R1 cycles** MV → Member → View:
  - **MV:** the song's camera;
  - **Member:** looks at one member's head; D-pad ←/→ switches members; L1 and L2 change distance and height; the arrows (left stick) orbit;
  - **View:** the whole stage from the front, same keys.
- **□** hides the whole HUD.
- **Needs:** a node's world position from `SceneModel` (the head bone).

## P4c: photos
- **○** takes a photo, up to 5 per live: the next frame is drawn without the HUD and read back from the GPU.
- **Readback:** `SDL_DownloadFromGPUTexture` of the swapchain into a transfer buffer.
- **The file:** written as PNG (stb_image_write, already in `third_party/stb`) at 1920 × 1080. A window of another size is scaled; *shortcut candidate:* the window's own size.
- **On screen:** the photo thumbnails in the HUD (`uip0015_PhotoNamber00`, `uip0016_ScreenShot00`).

## P4d: End the Live Show
- **The screen:** `uil0020_LiveEndLayout00` with the song, stage and costume, "Photos taken this time: n", the large preview, the five thumbnails, □ favourites and the D-pad.
- **The "What will you do next?" box:** Close the Window, Play Live Show Again, Return to Song Selection, Return to Main Menu.
- **Saving:** the photos go to `NextPhotoSlot` (P3), with the files and `photo` lines, then the "Save complete" dialog.
- **Close:** route checks of the files and save lines; PLAY.md and CHECKLIST.md items in P7.

**Order:** P4a → P4b → P4c → P4d. Each is built, tested and committed on its own when the user asks.
