# M14: Friendship, Wai-Wai! Boosters and birthday events

Date: 2026-11-19 07:02 (Asia/Jakarta). Survey: `analysis/202611190702-friendship-and-boosters/202611190702-analysis.md`.

**Goal:** lives give the chosen member Friendship Points, which raise her Friendship level and badges. Wai-Wai! Boosters are earned and can be used in Prepare for more materials. A member's birthday event runs around her birthday by the clock, collects points from lives and gives its rewards. The skills that act on these (M11: Friendship, Booster Boost, Drop Support) take effect.

**Exit test:**
- **Friendship:**
  - after a live, Items Obtained shows "Friendship Points Obtained" with the level bar, and the level rises by `s_level_datas`;
  - the points match the eboot's formula, with the Friendship skills;
  - the level is kept and shown where the game shows it (profile card heart, member popup or profile if any).
- **Badges:** reaching a Friendship rank (`s_emblemrank_datas`) gives its badge, choosable on the nameplate.
- **Boosters:**
  - the header and Prepare show the count;
  - □ in Prepare uses one, and the live then gives 10 more material units;
  - boosters are earned as in the game (drop pools, Drop Support, and whatever P1 finds).
- **Birthday events:**
  - with the clock in a member's window, Event → Ongoing Events shows the event and lives add points;
  - at 1000 the rewards (skill cards, key items) can be received;
  - the top-menu banner follows the event.
- **Missions:** the Friendship and booster mission kinds count.
- **Checks:** all routes and unit tests pass, and the asset scan is clean.
