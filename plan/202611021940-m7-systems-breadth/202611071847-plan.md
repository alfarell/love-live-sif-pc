# M7 finish plan: rewards, costumes and items, profile cards

Created: 202611071847 (Asia/Jakarta). The user decided "Finish M7 first" after the CP4 review (`plan/202610311813-port-approach-decision/202611071841-plan.md`). That review found two M7 parts not met, **costumes and items** and **profile cards**, plus open rewards.

## 1. What the data says (probed 2026-11-07, `work/m7/tables`, scripts `work/m7/*.py`)

- **Items:** `common/common.pac/item.pac/item_data.bin` `ll3::item::s_item_datas` has 62 rows of 40 bytes:
  - +0x00 id; +0x04 type (8 types: threads, cloths, key items, …);
  - +0x08 four u64 name offsets (Japanese, English, simplified Chinese, traditional Chinese);
  - the last row is "max".

  `item_combine_data.bin` (`s_itemCombineDatas`) is a 5 × 5 float table (1.0–1.5); *Hypothesis:* combine rates.
- **Costume recipes** are in `dress_data.bin`, row +0x58: u32 flag (1 = craftable, *Hypothesis*), 12 item ids, then 12 amounts. Example: dress 31 needs items 1, 4, 5, 6, 8, 13, 9, 21, 23, 29, 31 × 30, 35, 40, 40, 30, 20, 30, 35, 25, 22, 18.
- **Mission reward types** (rows per type, matched against the tables):

  | Type | Rows | Ids | Meaning |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 92 | 11019, 12022 … | *Hypothesis:* cards (not in `s_member_card_datas`; check `skill_card_datas`) |
  | 2 | 1679 | 1–60 | **items** (`s_item_datas` ids) |
  | 3 | 120 | always 5003 | *Hypothesis:* Wai-Wai! Booster |
  | 4 | 1651 | accolade ids, a few badge ids | **accolades** (and badges) |
  | 5 | 163 | 1–9222 | **profile-card frames** (all 163 in `s_profile_card_frame_datas`, 183 rows) |
  | 6 | 287 | 110471, 130201 … | **name plates** (`name_plate_%03d_%04d`) |
  | 7 | 66 | dress ids | **costumes** (all 66 in `s_dress_datas`) |

  So costumes come from missions and from crafting.

## 2. Steps (in this order; each ends with tests, captures, a note and a commit on request)

1. **Rewards and inventory.**
   - The save keeps claimed missions and what they gave: items with counts, accolades, badges, name plates, frames, costumes, boosters.
   - In the Task List, Enter on a completed mission claims it: GET! while it is claimable, COMPLETED once claimed. The tab shows how many are claimable.
   - The nameplate lists use the claimed awards, and claimed name plates become choosable.
2. **Costume ownership and crafting** (`30_costume`, top menu Costumes → Create/Change Costumes and Costume Materials).
   - Item reader and recipe reader.
   - A costume is owned if it is the base uniform, rewarded, or crafted. Crafting spends items.
   - The song select's costume step lists owned costumes only. Add-on costumes stay owned through their add-on.
3. **Per-member costumes.**
   - Change Costumes per member, saved.
   - The live dresses each member in their own choice when they have a model for it, else the song's pick. This needs the live scene to take one costume per member.
4. **Profile card screen** (`40_collection`, top menu Collection → Profile Card).
   - The game's card view with the nameplate (accolade, badge, name plate) and frame, chosen in the game's plate-change menu. This replaces the in-header edit.
5. **Badge levels:** what the badge rows' level and flags mean, and show them; or record why not.
6. **3- and 6-lane Button Controls:** only if the port can play the non-AC charts (check first); else record it as out of M7.
7. **M7 close:** a CP4 addendum with the times, and the roadmap.

Member and skill cards (type 1), boosters, events and rankings, the 3D navigator, story text (none in `talk.pac`).
