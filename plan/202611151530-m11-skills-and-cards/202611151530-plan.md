# M11: skill cards and member cards

Date: 2026-11-15 15:30 (Asia/Jakarta). Survey: `analysis/202611151530-skills-and-cards/202611151530-analysis.md`.

**Goal:** the player earns skill cards (from tasks and after lives), sees them in Collection → Skill Card, sets up to three for Play Live Show, and they work during and after the live as in the game. Member cards are shown with the costumes they belong to.

**Exit test:**
- **Live skills:** a home live with Judge Support set shows the skill firing (effect and voice) and widens the timing window for the stated seconds; the judge log proves the window. The firing rule matches the eboot.
- **Earning:** tasks give their skill cards; a live drop follows the eboot's rule; a second copy raises the level.
- **Screens:** Collection → Skill Card and the three-card set screen match shadPS4 captures in layout and texts.
- **Checks:** all routes and unit tests pass, and the asset scan is clean.

## Steps

### S1: eboot trace (no code)
- **Firing rule:** the readers of `s_skill_card_level_params` and of the level fields. Find out when a skill is tried (notes, time?), the chance (field a), field b, the duration (value) and "[Automatic activation]".
- **Effects:** what kinds 0, 1, 13, 14, 15, 17 and 38 do in the judge (`0xA0070` flags) and around it.
- **After the live:** where skill cards drop: the rate, which cards, the level rule for duplicates, and how kinds 24/25 change that.
- **Save:** the save layout of owned skill cards and the three set cards.
- **Output:** an analysis file. Anything not found is listed as blocked, with what is needed.

### S2: shadPS4 run (needs the user's approval)
- Play until a skill card is earned. Capture Collection → Skill Card, the set screen and where it opens, a live with a skill firing, and the result screen's drop.
- Check whether skills act in Play Live Show (AC).
- The member-card screens: Collection → Member Card, and the song select pictures.

### S3: data and save
- **Readers:** `src/game/card/` for skill cards (record, levels, texts in 4 languages) and member cards.
- **Save:** owned skill cards with their levels, and the three set cards per mode, if S1 shows per-mode sets.
- **Tasks:** reward type 1 grants a skill card. A duplicate follows S1's level rule.
- **Tests:** unit tests for the readers and the save round trip.

### S4: screens
- Collection → Skill Card (the list, with the card's details).
- The three-card set screen (`80_skill_card`), opened where S2 shows.
- Routes for both, with a seed save that owns some cards.

### S5: live skills
- **Firing:** the S1 rule, with the effect and voice (`s_skillEffect*`, `s_skillVoiceCues`).
- **Effects** in the judge: Judge Plus / Judge Support / Judge Support + (windows), Combo Support, Starting Combo Support, Long Notes Support, Finale Support.
- **Looks only:** Member Focus (camera), Shoutout (voice at the start), Special Effect Shower and Frame.
- **Tests and route:** unit tests on the judge with each skill; a route with a set skill.

### S6: after-live skills and drops
- **Drops:** skill card drops after a live, with Skill Drop Boost and Getting Skills Charge.
- **Costume materials:** kinds 27-35 change the M7 material drops (`RollDrops`).
- **Shortcut:** Friendship (kinds 18-21) and booster (22-23) skills have no effect until those systems exist. Their cards are shown and can be set.

### S6b: the game's material drop (added after S6, 2026-11-15)
- **Why:** S6 found that the material skills (kinds 27, 30-35) scale terms of the game's material drop (`FUN_000679a0`): a drop count from `s_CommonParam +0x150`, per-item caps, two passes. The port's `RollDrops` (M7) has none of these. See `analysis/202611151530-skills-and-cards/202611152318-analysis.md`.
- **Work:** identify the unknown inputs (result `+0x8c`, `s_CommonParam +0xf4/+0x1a8/+0x1f0`, `param_8`); port the function; then apply the material skills; tests on the counts and the skill factors.

### S7: member cards and close
- **Member cards:** the pictures in the song select's costume step, and Collection → Member Card, if S2 confirms the rule.
- **Close:** PLAY.md, a new CHECKLIST.md section, all routes and unit tests, the asset scan, the roadmap.

**Order:** S1 → S2 → S3 → S4 → S5 → S6 → S7. S1 and S2 can swap if the eboot trace stalls.

## Risks
- **The firing rule** may be hard to trace. *Fallback:* measure it in shadPS4 (S2), with the rule marked as a measured hypothesis.
- **Getting a skill card in shadPS4** needs a task or a drop. A task such as "play a song with Honoka" is cheap; the first-run tasks may already give one.
- **Size:** M11 is larger than M10. S5 and S6 can become separate milestones if needed.

**Not in M11:** Friendship levels, boosters, Birthday Events, the 3D navigator, Create a New Profile Card.
