## Decision

- The corrections become a dedicated milestone, **M7.5 "Live play fidelity"**, which runs **before M8**.
- M8 (video, first-run setup, build-and-run docs) and M9 stay as they are. They start after M7.5.
- The roadmap is updated to match (`plan/202610311831-sif-port-roadmap/202610311831-plan.md`, milestone table and checklist).

- the indicators and normal notes;

- the combo.

Unit tests cover the collection and finale rules. Debug and Release are rebuilt. Each open question (Q1-Q6 of the analysis) is either answered or written down as a marked shortcut.

### G1 Note and indicator sprites (items 1 and 2.1)

- **Current state:**
  - the indicator is `ui_ingame_notes` (1, 0);
  - the note is (group, 1);
  - there are no icons.
- **Expected state:**
  - the indicator is `ui_ingame_judgering_all_mark` (0, 0) with the lane's button icon (two icons in the centre lane);
  - the normal note is the pink ring (1, 0) over the pink fill (2, 0), with the icon.
- **Required changes:**
  - **Textures:** load `ui_ingame_judgering_all_mark`.
  - **Sprite table:** a small table of named cells (indicator, note ring, note fill, finale disc per group, icon mark per group, sparkle, bars, brackets, icons). This replaces the loose `ring`/`note`/`trail` meshes.
  - **Icons:** taken from `PadSlots`, the lane's pad button, mapped to an icon cell.
  - **Sizes:** re-measure them against the 152 px layout ring.
- **Affected:** `src/app/live_screen.cpp` (`Playfield::Load`, the draw loop); `src/app/input.h` (read only: `PadSlots`).
- **Shortcut to note:** the "Rhythm Icon Button Display" option is not built. Icons are always on.
- **Check:** a capture next to screenshot 1.

### G2 Judgement text per lane and combo (items 5 and 6)

- **Current state:** one judgement text at (960, 470); the combo opaque at the same place.
- **Expected state:**
  - each lane plays its own judgement `.fad` at its ring. The text sits about 97 px above the ring centre, as in screenshot 2;
  - the combo is lower and at about 40% alpha.
- **Required changes:**

  - drop `judged` and `judged_at`;
  - `DrawCombo` gets y and alpha.
- **Affected:** `live_screen.cpp` (draw loop, `DrawCombo`).
- **Note:** the combo position is the user's choice, as Q6 is open. Start at y ≈ 530 and adjust on a capture.

### G3 Hold lines (item 3)

- **Current state:** a disc trail every 40 ms and an end disc.
- **Expected state:** a straight line from the head to the tail made of `connect_note` (`connect_note_finale` in the finale); white while waiting, pink while `Judge::Held`.
- **Required changes:**
  - a stretched-quad helper. Reuse the maths in `DrawBar`, which already stretches a cell between two points;
  - load the two `common_tex` textures.
- **Affected:** `live_screen.cpp`.
- **Shortcut to note:** pink is a tint on the white strip (*Hypothesis*), and the end cap follows Q4.

### G4 Simultaneous groups (item 4)

- **Current state:** `DrawBar` joins neighbouring notes of equal time with one long bar.
- **Expected state:**
  - each note of a group has the brackets and the short side bars;
  - the colour alternates orange, blue, orange … per group in song order;
  - when every note of the group is pressed or held together, its indicators show the vertical line.
- **Required changes:**
  - **Game logic:** `live::Chart` gets a group index per note (notes within 1 ms share a group, numbered in time order). This goes in `chart.cpp`, which is generic and testable.
  - **Screen:** draw per note in `live_screen.cpp`.
  - **Satisfied check:** all notes of the group in state Holding or Done with a grade better than Miss. Read from `Judge`; no new judge rules.
- **Affected:** `src/game/live/chart.{h,cpp}`, `live_screen.cpp`, `tests/unit` (the group test).
- **Depends on:** Q3 for the vertical-line asset. Until it is known, a marked shortcut uses the bar cell turned 90°.

### G5 Finale Rhythm Icons and LOVELIVE! (item 2.2)

- **Current state:** channels 2-5 are not read, and the HUD marks stay grey.
- **Expected state:**
  - nine notes per play carry the group's icon mark;
  - each hit icon lights the next letter in `uip0003_FFmark`, using the `ingame_ffmark_<group>` cells (L O V E L I V E !);
  - the grey symbol cell is the empty state.
- **Required changes:**
  - **Chart:** `live::LoadSong` also reads `_ch2`-`_ch5` into `Chart::icon_sets` (3 sets × 9 times and lanes) and `Chart::phases` (3 phases plus the finale start).
  - **Rules:** a new `live::Finale` object in `src/game/live/` that:
    - picks the set per phase (rule from Q1);
    - marks the icon notes;
    - counts collected icons from the judgement log;
    - reports `letters` and `ready`.

    It stays generic: no drawing.
  - **Screen:** the icon mark on those notes; `hud.SetMaterialUv` per `M_FFmark_0k`; show `uip0007_FFmark_ef` when a letter is lit.
- **Affected:** `src/game/live/chart.{h,cpp}`, `song.{h,cpp}`, a new `finale.{h,cpp}`, `live_screen.cpp`, `tests/unit` (a new `finale_test` with a fixed chart), `docs/formats/scd.md`.

### G6 Finale section and score (item 2.3)

- **Current state:** the finale look is drawn all song; there is no finale score.
- **Expected state:**
  - from the `ch5` note-69 time, if all nine letters are lit, the finale starts:
    - the `uip0008_FinaleON00` banner;
    - notes drawn as the yellow group disc with ring, icon and sparkles;
    - `connect_note_finale` for holds;
    - finale particles;
  - finale notes score with the factor from Q2;
  - before that, and when the icons were not all collected, notes stay normal.
- **Required changes:**
  - `live::Judge` gets a per-note score factor (default 1.0) that `live::Finale` sets for notes in the finale section;
  - `MaxScore` keeps the all-Perfect value without the finale (*Hypothesis*, see the analysis §7);
  - the screen switches the sprites by `Finale::active`.
- **Affected:** `src/game/live/judge.{h,cpp}`, `finale.{h,cpp}`, `live_screen.cpp`, `tests/unit` (judge and finale tests: score with and without the finale).
- **Regression check:** existing judge tests unchanged (factor 1.0); autoplay scores on songs with a finale rise. Note the before and after numbers.

## Regressions to watch

- Chart and song loading is shared with `sif_sweep`, the live test and the result screen. Keep `ch1` behaviour identical; channels 2-5 are optional, so a missing file means no finale.
- Score and rank change only through the finale factor. Without `ch5` the score is the same as before.
- Note sizes change with the new cells; check that the lane positions still match the layout (152 px rings).
- Pad mapping changes must refresh the icons at live start.
- Frame cost: more sprites per note (ring, fill, icon, marks). Compare `live frame cost` in the log before and after.
