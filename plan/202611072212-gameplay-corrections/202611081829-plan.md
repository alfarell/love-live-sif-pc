Created: 202611081829 (Asia/Jakarta). Follows `202611072212-plan.md` and the G0 results (`analysis/202611072212-gameplay-feedback/202611081816-analysis.md`).

**User decision (2026-11-08):** the "vertical line" of a satisfied simultaneous press means the vertical parts of the brackets. No extra asset is needed for it.

## Conventions

- Paths are inside the game dump (read in place, never copied).
- **Cell (c, r):** column c, row r of an atlas, counted from 0.
- The two atlases:
  - `ui_ingame_notes`: 1280 × 1536, 5 × 6 cells of 256 px. Sheet: `work/m6/ingame/ui_ingame_notes.png`.
  - `ui_ingame_judgering_all_mark`: 1024 × 1280, 4 × 5 cells of 256 px. Sheet: `work/gameplay-fix/ui_ingame_judgering_all_mark_bg.png`.
- **Status:**
  - **Seen:** matches the screenshots or shadPS4 frames.
  - **Hyp.:** best match, to confirm when the step is built.
  - **Kept:** already in the port, unchanged.
